## Real-server finding: the terrain surface was cut along the wrong diagonal (T4, 2026-10-23, Claude Code)

- **Evidence.** Run `analysis/native-runs/2026-10-23-gameplay-real-two-e` (`mixed.move`, walk-to-meet): the original server sent the target 20 `ReadClientMove` corrections, 8 within 3 m of Unity [6390, 430, -3730] (a rocky gully on a ~40 degree slope; 9 with [6372, 436, -3749] on the same slope), 8 at [5002, 428, -3583], one each at [6357, 426, -3793], [5135, 441, -3468] and [5602, 410, -3473]; no kick. All 20 carry a normal transit time (33-76 ms, `lateS`), and a `VerifyRPC` late drop answers with the transit time of a move at least ~1 s late (`HumanController.GetClientMove`), so every one is a TruthDetector blocked-trace rollback: each puts the target back on the move before the newest (`Test_MovementTrace` rollback to the previous accepted point). `tools/replay-truth-trace.mjs` replayed 0 of them and nothing in the gateway world (scene colliders, trees, RockSpawn rocks, resource spawners, NGC) lies within 1.2 m of the rays: the nearest solid at the 6390 spot is a RockSpawn rock 2.3 m away, and at 5002 there is nothing within 3.5 m.
- **Cause.** `Physics.Raycast(old + 0.5 m up, new - old, |new - old|, 538444803)` includes layer 19, the terrain. Unity splits every heightmap cell (7.8 m here) along the (x,z)-(x+1,z+1) diagonal: `Heightmap::GetInterpolatedHeight`, the PhysX TerrainCollider and the terrain mesh. `browser/terrain.mjs sampleTriangle`, `makeTerrain` and `terrain-lod.mjs` used the other diagonal, so on convex cells the port's ground (where the motor puts the feet, 1 mm above it in the recorded moves) lay under the server's by up to half the cross-diagonal difference: 1.35 m mid-cell at the 6390 spot, 0.89 m at 5002, and over 0.5 m in one cell in eight of the island's 4.2 M (p99 2.6 m; concave cells were the other way round, the player floating). On those steep creases the server's trace from 0.5 m above the feet went into the real terrain. The heights were also normalised by 32767 instead of Unity's `kMaxHeight` 32766 (1.3 cm low at 430 m).
- **Proof.** With Unity's diagonal and 32766, (1) all 29,050 terrain `TreeInstance`s (snapped to `GetInterpolatedHeight` by the editor) sit on the recovered surface to 0.1 mm (old surface: median 2.1 cm, max 1.44 m); (2) the trace reproduces every rollback of the three real runs with moves.json: two-e 20/20, two-d 8/8, two-c 3/3 (two-c's "not a physical block" was this too), with 0 of 5,406 accepted moves blocked. Blocked rays dip 1.4 mm to 0.24 m into the surface; accepted ones clear it by at least 9 mm. No other hypothesis (bilinear, old diagonal with either scale) explains any of the 31.
- **Fix.** `browser/terrain.mjs` `cellHeight`/`sampleTriangle` and `makeTerrain`, `browser/terrain-lod.mjs` (`coarseHeight`, `chunkGeometry` indices a,b,d / a,d,c) use Unity's split, so the motor, the gateway's trace world (`bridge/line-of-sight.mjs`), the walk planner and the drawn ground share the server's surface. `tools/export_world.py` writes `heights.f32` as sample / 32766 * 1500 and validates trees on the triangle surface (max 0.08 mm; asserts < 5 mm); `RLB_EXPORT_OUT` added. `tools/truth-detector.mjs losTrace` samples the terrain every 2 cm with no slack (was 0.25 m with 0.1 m slack for the old error). Shared data: `heights.f32` and `manifest.json` replaced in `browser/assets/recovered` (backup `analysis/recovered-backups/world-2026-10-23-terrain-diagonal/`).
- **Effect.** Offline (`tests/terrain-surface.test.mjs`, the harness walker sprinting on `LegacyMotor` + the TruthDetector model) the port's motor walks both spots with 0 blocked traces; on the former surface the same walks gave 12 blocked traces and two kicks ("violation 595", "violation 496"), the pattern of the unexplained "kicked for violation 592" of run two (whose target stream was never saved, so it stays probable, not proven).
