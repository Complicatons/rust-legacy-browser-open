The fastest way to reach us is our Discord server.

DiscordHelp, questions and news. For anything private, open a ticket in #open-a-ticket. GitHubThe public development journal: progress, screenshots and the roadmap.

What to contact us about

  • Help with your account or the game: ask in Discord, or open a ticket if it involves your account.
  • Bugs and cheaters: report them in Discord. In game, use /report to report a player to the server's admins.
  • Running a server: see List your server and the help pages, then ask in Discord.
  • Your data (a copy, a correction, deleting your account or your email from the opening-day list): open a ticket. We'll reply within one month. See the Privacy notice.

A contact email address will be added here once the site's email is set up.
