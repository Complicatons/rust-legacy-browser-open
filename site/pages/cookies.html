Cookies

Last updated 22 October 2026

No tracking, no adverts, no analytics. We only use cookies that sign-in and account security need, so there's no cookie banner to click through: the law doesn't ask for consent for these. Everything else the site remembers stays in your own browser.

Cookies

NameWhat it's forHow long

rlb_deviceRecognises this browser so banned players can't simply make a new account (set once you're signed in; we store only a hash of it). See the Privacy notice.2 years

All four are first-party, can't be read by scripts on the page, and are only sent over HTTPS. Cloudflare, which protects the site, may set its own security cookie when it needs to check a visitor is human.

Settings kept in your browser

These never leave your computer (they're in your browser's local storage, not sent to us):

  • Your game options: graphics and sound settings, key bindings, console settings and quality presets.
  • Server browser favourites and history, and the server you're joining.
  • Single-player saves on the offline island.
  • Small website choices: menu music on or off, the live title-screen background, and dismissed notices.

One exception is sent to us: a copy of the rlb_device token (rlb-device), so a browser that cleared its cookies is still recognised, and when it was last sent.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of these and signs you out.
