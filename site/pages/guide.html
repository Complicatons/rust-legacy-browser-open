    Troubleshooting

    The mouse won't turn the camera
    Click the game once to capture the mouse. Esc gives it back.
    I want the mouse back
    Esc always gives it back. You can also pick F2, F3 or F4 as a release key in Options.
    WebGL is off
    Turn on hardware acceleration in the browser settings and restart the browser.
    Ctrl+W closed the tab
    The browser keeps shortcuts like Ctrl+W and Ctrl+T, and the game can't block them. Crouch is Ctrl, so moving forward while crouched can close the tab: rebind Crouch in Options.
    I want fullscreen
    Alt+Enter, or tick Fullscreen in Options.
