You wear four pieces: helmet, vest, pants and boots. Their protection **adds up**, separately for each kind of damage.

![A full Kevlar set in the armour panel](/site/generated/media/guides/armour-kevlar.jpg)

## How protection works

Damage of a kind is cut by **protection ÷ 200**:

| Total protection | Damage you take |
|---|---|
| 0 | 100% |
| 50 | 75% |
| 100 | 50% |
| 150 | 25% |
| 200 | 0% |

| Set | Bullet | Melee | Explosion | Radiation | Cold |

| Rad suit | 20 | 20 | 95 | 140 | 100 |

A full Kevlar set takes 42.5% off every bullet. A rad suit is for the radioactive compounds, not for fights (see [Radiation](/wiki/guides/radiation)). Mixing works: armour is just added up.
