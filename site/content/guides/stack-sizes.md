Space runs out fast, so it helps to know what stacks and how far.

![The inventory](/site/generated/media/guides/inventory.jpg)

## How many fit in one slot

| Per slot | Items |
|---|---|
| **250** | Every resource ([[item:Wood]], [[item:Stones]], [[item:Metal Fragments]], [[item:Sulfur]], [[item:Gunpowder]], [[item:Cloth]], [[item:Leather]] and the rest), all ammo except arrows, empty and primed casings, food and drink, [[item:Anti-Radiation Pills]], every building piece, [[item:Camp Fire]], [[item:Sleeping Bag]] and [[item:Wood Barricade]] |
| **12** | Weapon parts and armour parts |
| **10** | [[item:Arrow]] and [[item:Flare]] |
| **5** | [[item:Bandage]], [[item:Small Medkit]], [[item:Large Medkit]], [[item:Explosive Charge]], [[item:F1 Grenade]], doors, [[item:Small Stash]], [[item:Wood Storage Box]], [[item:Large Wood Storage]], [[item:Wood Shelter]] and [[item:Metal Window Bars]] |
| **1** | Weapons, tools, armour, weapon mods, blueprints, and the bigger deployables ([[item:Furnace]], [[item:Workbench]], [[item:Repair Bench]], [[item:Bed]] and others) |

Arrows stacking to only 10 is easy to miss: a full quiver of 40 takes 4 slots.

## Merging and splitting

- **Merge**: drag a stack onto another stack of the same item. It fills that stack up to the limit and leaves the rest where it was.
- **Split**: right-click a stack and choose *Split*. Half (rounded down) moves to the first empty slot. You need an empty slot, and only stacks of 2 or more can be split.
- New items you pick up or craft fill your existing stacks first, then empty slots.

Weapons, tools and armour never stack, because each one keeps its own condition and ammo.
