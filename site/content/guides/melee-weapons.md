Your gathering tools are also your first weapons. Each one reaches **1.5 m**, so you have to be right next to what you hit.

![The pick axe in hand](/site/generated/media/guides/weapon-pickaxe.jpg)

## Damage and speed

| Tool | Damage | Seconds per swing | Damage per second | Calories per swing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[item:Rock]] | 20 | 1.95 | 10 | 2 |
| [[item:Stone Hatchet]] | 15 | 0.9 | 17 | 5 |
| [[item:Hatchet]] | 25 | 0.9 | **28** | 5 |
| [[item:Pick Axe]] | **60** | 2 | **30** | 10 |

The pick axe hits hardest and the hatchet is nearly as good while swinging more than twice as often, so it's easier to land. Calories per swing vary by 20% either way.

## Hits to kill

Without armour, a player has 100 health:

| Tool | Player | Wolf (125) | Bear (250) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rock | 5 | 7 | 13 |
| Stone Hatchet | 7 | 9 | 17 |
| Hatchet | 4 | 5 | 10 |
| Pick Axe | 2 | 3 | 5 |

A hit does the same damage wherever it lands: only bullets get a headshot bonus. Animals have no armour. Players' armour cuts melee damage: see [Armour](/wiki/guides/armour).

## Starving

With no calories left, every swing takes **twice as long**. Eat before a fight (see [Food and cooking](/wiki/guides/food-and-cooking)).

## Wear

Every swing that hits something has a 1-in-4 chance to take **2.5%** off the tool's condition, so a tool lasts about 160 hits on average. The [[item:Rock]] never wears out. Fix worn tools at a repair bench (see [Repairing](/wiki/guides/repairing)).
