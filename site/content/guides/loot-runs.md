The fastest way to guns and blueprints is a run through the compounds, where 80 crate spots refill as they're looted. The catch is radiation.

## Where to go

Turn on *Crates* and *Radiation* on the [Island map](/island) to see every crate spot and zone.

| Place | Crate spots | Rads per minute at the centre |
|---|---|---|
| [[place:Smokestack Compound]] | about 19 | 1,000 |
| [[place:Warehouse Compound]] | about 14 | 3,000 |
| [[place:Oil Tanks]] | about 10 | 1,000 |
| [[place:Water Tank]] | about 6 | none: it sits just outside the Oil Tanks zones |
| Hangars | 8 and 14 | 500 |
| Bunkers | 2–5 each | 0–500 |

Each crate is a roll of the dice: 55% junk box, 18% ammo, 18% medical, 9% weapons. See [Loot crates](/wiki/guides/loot-crates) for what's inside.

## How long you can stay

Radiation only hurts above **500 rads**. Rads also wear off while you stand in a zone, so how long it takes to reach 500 depends on how strong the spot is. Standing at the very centre:

| Place | No protection | Full rad suit |
|---|---|---|
| Warehouse Compound | **10 s** | 40 s |
| Smokestack, Oil Tanks | 35 s | 3½ min |
| Hangars | 1 min 23 s | **never** |

Zones are weaker away from their centre, so the edges give you much longer. Above 500 you take damage and stop healing until your rads come back down.

## What to bring

- A **rad suit** (all four pieces block 70% of the radiation) or at least Kevlar pants and boots and any leather.
- **[[item:Anti-Radiation Pills]]**: 200 rads each, taken off over time. [[item:Small Rations]] take off 300.
- A weapon: **mutant wolves and bears** live around the compounds, and so do other players. Mutants are worth killing: each one drops a loot sack that can hold a blueprint or research kit (see [Animals and hunting](/wiki/guides/animals-and-hunting)).
- A stack of [[item:Bandage]], and empty inventory space.

## Planning the route

- **Hangars first** if you have no rad suit: 500 a minute is the gentlest.
- **The Warehouse Compound** only with a suit and pills, in and out.
- Leave the zone to let rads drop, then go back in: they fall faster the higher they are, and twice as fast next to a burning fire.
- A crate spot only refills after its old crate is emptied, at its next check, which comes every 10–20 minutes. Arriving just after someone else usually means a wait.

Better than any crate: an [airdrop](/wiki/guides/airdrops).
