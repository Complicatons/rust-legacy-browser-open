The rule of the island: **the better the loot, the stronger the radiation** (and the more mutant animals around it).

## Reading the map

The [Island map](/island) has a 16 × 16 grid (A–P across, 1–16 down) and these layers:

| Layer | Shows |
|---|---|
| Radiation | the 12 radiation zones |
| Crates | the 80 fixed crate spots |
| Mutants | the 16 mutant animal zones |
| Animals | the 16 wildlife zones |
| Resources | where wood piles and ore nodes spawn |
| No-build | the 13 circles where nothing can be placed |
| Elevation | height lines every 25 m and the coastline |
| Spawns | the 63 places new players appear |

Click a crate spot or a zone on the map to see what spawns there. The height under your mouse is shown in the corner.

![The Hangar](/site/generated/media/guides/place-hangar.jpg)

## Where to live

- Away from the compounds: fewer players pass by.
- Near wood and ore: turn on *Resources*.
- Not in a no-build circle, and not on a spawn point if you want quiet neighbours.
