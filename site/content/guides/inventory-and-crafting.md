Press **Tab** to open your inventory. The six slots along the bottom are your **belt**; keys **1–6** use or hold whatever is in them.

![The inventory](/site/generated/media/guides/inventory.jpg)

## Crafting

The list on the right shows every blueprint you know. Pick one to see its ingredients. Green numbers mean you have enough, red mean you don't. Use **+** and **−** (Shift or Ctrl for bigger steps) to craft several at once, then **Craft**. You can keep playing while it works and cancel at any time.

## Workbench recipes

Anything made of [[item:Low Quality Metal]], all guns and explosives need you to stand at a [[item:Workbench]] (8 stones, 50 wood). The crafting window says **REQUIRES WORKBENCH** in red until you are.

Standing at a workbench also makes **everything** craft **3 times faster**, even recipes that don't need one. Servers can change that speed. Walk away mid-craft and it slows back down; a recipe that needs the bench is cancelled.

## Moving items

Drag to move. Drop a stack onto the same item to merge it. Right-click an item for its options: *Split*, *Eat*, *Use*, *Study* a blueprint, or *Unload* a gun.
