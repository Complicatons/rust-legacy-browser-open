A base keeps your loot and gives you somewhere safe to log off. Every piece snaps to a grid of **2.5 m** across and **4 m** up.

![A one-room hut with a door and a roof](/site/generated/media/guides/build-hut.jpg)

## The order

1. **[[item:Wood Foundation]]**: a 5 m square on the ground. Everything else sits on foundations.
2. **[[item:Wood Pillar]]**: at the corners. Walls go *between* two pillars, and a ceiling needs a pillar at each of its 4 corners.
3. **[[item:Wood Wall]]**, **[[item:Wood Doorway]]** or **[[item:Wood Window]]** between the pillars.
4. **[[item:Wood Ceiling]]** on top: a roof, or the floor of the next storey.
5. **[[item:Wood Stairs]]** or a **[[item:Wood Ramp]]** to go up.

Hold the piece, aim where it goes (a green preview means it fits) and click. You can place pieces up to 8 m away, ceilings up to 4 m. Pieces can be rotated before you place them.

![The green placement preview of a foundation](/site/generated/media/guides/build-preview.jpg)

## Wood pieces

All wood pieces are made from [[item:Wood Planks]] (10 wood each, 2 seconds).

| Piece | Planks | Health |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 8 | 2,500 |
| Pillar | 2 | 5,000 |
| Wall | 4 | 1,000 |
| Doorway | 4 | 1,000 |
| Window | 4 | 1,000 |
| Ceiling | 6 | 1,000 |
| Stairs | 5 | 1,000 |
| Ramp | 5 | 1,000 |

## Metal pieces

Metal pieces use [[item:Low Quality Metal]] instead of planks and must be made at a [[item:Workbench]]. Walls, doorways, windows and the foundation have twice the health of wood:

| Piece | Low quality metal | Health |
|---|---|---|
| [[item:Metal Foundation]] | 8 | 5,000 |
| [[item:Metal Pillar]] | 2 | 5,000 |
| [[item:Metal Wall]] | 4 | 2,000 |
| [[item:Metal Doorway]] | 4 | 2,000 |
| [[item:Metal Window]] | 4 | 2,000 |
| [[item:Metal Ceiling]] | 6 | 1,000 |
| [[item:Metal Stairs]] | 5 | 1,000 |
| [[item:Metal Ramp]] | 5 | 1,000 |

Nobody starts knowing the metal pieces, and their blueprints never drop. To learn one, get the piece itself (airdrops have a bundle of metal building parts) and [research it](/wiki/guides/research-and-blueprints) with a research kit.

## What can hurt a building

- **Guns and melee can't damage walls, doorways, windows, foundations, pillars or ceilings at all.**
- **Explosives** can destroy walls, doorways and windows, but not foundations, pillars or ceilings. See [Raiding](/wiki/guides/raiding).
- Stairs and ramps can be chipped down with melee weapons.
- Doors are another story: see [Doors and code locks](/wiki/guides/doors-and-locks).

Left alone long enough, every building also [decays](/wiki/guides/decay).

## Where you can't build

Some places around the monuments are **no-build zones** (13 circles across the island). Turn on *No-build* on the [Island map](/island) before you pick a spot.
