Damage is per bullet. Shotguns fire several pellets per shot.

## Headshots and silencers

- A **headshot** does **3×** damage to players, and **8×** to animals.
- A **silenced** gun does **60%** of its normal damage (see [Weapon mods](/wiki/guides/weapon-mods)).
- Armour cuts bullet damage further (see [Armour](/wiki/guides/armour)).

## Making guns

Every gun needs a [[item:Workbench]] and [[item:Low Quality Metal]]: an [[item:M4]] takes 30, an [[item:MP5A4]] 20, a [[item:P250]] or [[item:Shotgun]] 12. Find one in a crate first and [research it](/wiki/guides/research-and-blueprints) to make your own. Ammo recipes are in [Gunpowder and explosives](/wiki/guides/gunpowder-and-explosives).
