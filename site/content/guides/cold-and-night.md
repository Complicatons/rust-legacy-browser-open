Nights on the island are nearly black. That's not a graphics setting, it's how the original lighting works.

![Night with no light](/site/generated/media/guides/night-dark.jpg)

## The cold

At night you get **cold** unless you're warm:

- Standing near a burning [[item:Camp Fire]] or [[item:Furnace]] keeps you warm.
- Clothing with **cold protection** keeps you warm: every 20 points of cold protection you wear cancel the night's cold completely.

While cold you burn an extra 1,500 calories an hour and **you don't heal on your own**.

| Clothing | Cold |
|---|---|
| Rad suit (each piece) | 25 |
| [[item:Leather Vest]], [[item:Leather Helmet]] | 15 |
| Cloth, Kevlar, other leather pieces | 5–10 |

A [[item:Cloth Vest]] and [[item:Cloth Pants]] (15 together) aren't quite enough on their own; add a [[item:Cloth Helmet]] and you're at 20.

## Light

- [[item:Torch]]: 1 low grade fuel, 1 wood, 1 cloth. It lights itself when you hold it.
- [[item:Flare]]: throw it to light up an area.
- [[item:Flashlight Mod]]: fits on a gun (see [Weapon mods](/wiki/guides/weapon-mods)).

![Holding a torch](/site/generated/media/guides/night-torch.jpg)

Remember that a light shows everyone where you are.
