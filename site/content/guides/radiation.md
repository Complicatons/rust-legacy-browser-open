The compounds with the best loot are radioactive. Rads build up while you stand in a zone, and the **Rads** number in the corner shows yours.

![Standing in the Warehouse Compound's radiation](/site/generated/media/guides/radiation.jpg)

## The zones

There are 12 zones. The strongest are the compounds:

| Place | Rads per minute | Zone radius |
|---|---|---|
| [[place:Warehouse Compound]] | 3,000 | 90 m |
| [[place:Smokestack Compound]] | 1,000 | 200 m |
| [[place:Oil Tanks]] (two zones side by side) | 1,000 | 120 m each |
| Both hangars and a barn | 500 | 75–100 m |
| A bunker | 250 | 100 m |

Four more 500 zones sit in open ground between the compounds. The [[place:Water Tank]] is just outside the Oil Tanks zones: the rads there come from walking through them.

Every zone is at full strength in its middle and fades to nothing at its edge. They're all on the [Island map](/island) (turn on *Radiation*), and the [Radiation](/wiki/radiation) section lists each one.

## What rads do

- Under 500 rads: no harm yet.
- Over 500: **radiation poisoning** hurts you every few seconds, and you stop healing on your own, until your rads drop back under 500.
- The most you can carry is 3,000.

Rads wear off by themselves. The higher they are, the faster they fall, and **twice as fast next to a burning fire**, where poisoning also hurts a third as much.

## Getting rid of rads

| Item | Removes |
|---|---|
| [[item:Anti-Radiation Pills]] | 200 |
| [[item:Small Rations]] | 300 |
| [[item:Small Water Bottle]] | 75 |
| [[item:Can of Beans]] | 50 |
| [[item:Can of Tuna]] | 25 |

Anti-rads work over time, at up to 3,000 a minute.

## Protection

Radiation protection cuts the rads you pick up by **protection ÷ 200**: 100 protection halves them.

| Piece | Radiation |
|---|---|
| [[item:Rad Suit Vest]] | 50 |
| [[item:Rad Suit Pants]] | 40 |
| [[item:Rad Suit Helmet]] | 30 |
| [[item:Rad Suit Boots]] | 20 |
| Leather pieces, Kevlar pants and boots | 10 each |

A full rad suit adds up to 140, which blocks 70% of the radiation. That turns the Warehouse Compound's 3,000 a minute at the centre into 900. Mutant wolves and bears also spawn around the radioactive compounds (see [Animals and hunting](/wiki/guides/animals-and-hunting)).
