Four mods fit on guns. Drag a mod onto a gun in your inventory to fit it.

![Aiming down a holo sight](/site/generated/media/guides/weapon-holo.jpg)

## The mods

| Mod | What it does | Recipe (workbench) |
|---|---|---|
| [[item:Holo sight]] | A red-dot sight: aiming zooms in a little and recoil kick is 40% smaller | 5 low quality metal |
| [[item:Laser Sight]] | A red laser beam where you're pointing. Toggle with **G** | 5 low quality metal |
| [[item:Flashlight Mod]] | A torch on the gun. Toggle with **F** | 5 low quality metal |
| [[item:Silencer]] | Quieter shots, no muzzle flash, no visible tracer, but **60% damage** | 8 low quality metal |

The [[item:Shotgun]] is the odd one out: it still shows its flash and tracers with a silencer on.

## Mod slots

Every gun rolls its number of mod slots when it is made or found: anywhere from **none up to 5** (up to 4 on the [[item:Bolt Action Rifle]]), so two M4s can have different slots. The [[item:Pipe Shotgun]] and [[item:HandCannon]] never take any. A gun with more slots is worth keeping. Each gun's item page in the wiki shows its maximum.

![A flashlight and laser on an M4 at night](/site/generated/media/guides/night-flashlight.jpg)

## When to use what

- **Night**: flashlight and laser, but remember both give you away.
- **Long range**: holo sight.
- **Ambushes and quiet raids**: silencer, if you can live with the damage loss.
