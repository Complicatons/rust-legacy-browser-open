Crates are the fastest way to get guns, blueprints and medical supplies before you can make them. Only the **Weapon Loot Box** holds blueprints.

## Where they spawn

There are **80 fixed crate spots**, all at the compounds and buildings. Turn on *Crates* on the [Island map](/island) to see every one.

| Place | Crate spots nearby |
|---|---|
| [[place:Smokestack Compound]] | about 19 |
| [[place:Warehouse Compound]] | about 14 |
| [[place:Oil Tanks]] | about 10 |
| Hangars | 8 and 14 |
| Bunkers | 2–5 each |

The best spots are radioactive (see [Radiation](/wiki/guides/radiation)).

![The Smokestack Compound, where most crates spawn](/site/generated/media/guides/place-smokestack-compound.jpg)

## Which crate you get

Every spot rolls a new crate each time:

| Crate | Chance | What's inside |
|---|---|---|
| Box Loot | 55% | 1–2 picks from junk: ammo, cloth, gunpowder, metal, paper, flares |
| Ammo Loot Box | 18% | 1 pick from the ammo list |
| Medical Loot Box | 18% | 1–2 picks from the medical list |
| Weapon Loot Box | 9% | 1 pick from the weapon list |

Each crate has 12 slots. The [Loot crate pages](/wiki/resources) list every item and its chance per pick.

## Respawning

A spot only spawns a new crate after its old one is gone. Every **10–20 minutes** it checks: if the last crate has been emptied and removed, a new one appears. Crates disappear once they're empty.

## Research kits and blueprints

- **Blueprints**: only Weapon Loot Boxes (40% of their rolls) and [Mutant Loot Sacks](/wiki/resources/mutant-loot-sack). See [Research and blueprints](/wiki/guides/research-and-blueprints).
- **Research kits**: Ammo Loot Boxes (5.5% per roll) and Mutant Loot Sacks (5.8% of kills).

## Mutant loot sacks

Every mutant wolf or bear you kill drops a sack beside its body with one roll of loot, including blueprints, research kits and leather armour. It disappears after 5 minutes. See [Animals and hunting](/wiki/guides/animals-and-hunting).

## Airdrops

Supply crates from airdrops are much better for building and raiding gear (explosive charges, metal doors, a large medkit), but they never hold blueprints. See [Airdrops](/wiki/guides/airdrops).
