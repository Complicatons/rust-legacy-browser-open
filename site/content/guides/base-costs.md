Every wood piece is made from [[item:Wood Planks]] (10 wood each) and every metal piece from [[item:Low Quality Metal]] (15 metal fragments each, at a workbench). Here is what each piece really costs in raw materials.

## Per piece

| Piece | Wood version | Metal version |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 80 wood | 120 fragments |
| Pillar | 20 wood | 30 fragments |
| Wall, doorway or window | 40 wood | 60 fragments |
| Ceiling | 60 wood | 90 fragments |
| Stairs or ramp | 50 wood | 75 fragments |
| Door | 30 wood | 200 fragments |

Doors are crafted directly: the [[item:Wooden Door]] from wood and the [[item:Metal Door]] from fragments, with no planks or metal in between. Defences: [[item:Wood Barricade]] 30 wood, [[item:Spike Wall]] 100 wood, [[item:Large Spike Wall]] 200 wood, [[item:Metal Window Bars]] 100 fragments. A [[item:Wood Gate]] is 120 wood and a [[item:Wood Gateway]] 400.

## A first hut

![A one-room hut](/site/generated/media/guides/build-hut.jpg)

One foundation, four pillars, three walls, a doorway, a ceiling and a door:

| | Wood hut | Metal hut |
|---|---|---|
| Materials | **410 wood** | **770 metal fragments** |
| Planks or metal to craft first | 38 planks | 38 low quality metal |
| Crafting time | 5 min 16 s (1 min 45 s at a workbench) | 1 min 45 s (it has to be at a workbench) |

The metal hut also needs a [[item:Workbench]] (8 stones, 50 wood) to craft at, and 770 fragments is a lot of smelting: at 3–8 fragments per [[item:Metal Ore]], expect to smelt about 140 ore (see [Smelting](/wiki/guides/smelting)).

## Making it bigger

Two foundations side by side share a wall's worth of pillars:

| | Wood |
|---|---|
| 1×1 hut | 410 wood |
| 2×1 hut (2 foundations, 6 pillars, 5 walls, a doorway, 2 ceilings, a door) | **670 wood** |
| Each extra floor on a 1×1 (4 pillars, 4 walls, a ceiling) | 300 wood, plus 50 for stairs |

## Saving time

Crafting at a [[item:Workbench]] is **3 times faster**, even for wood pieces that don't need one. Put the bench down first and build the rest next to it. Craft all the planks you need in one batch: they take 2 seconds each.
