Buildings and things you place slowly fall apart unless someone uses them. This stops abandoned bases from filling up the island.

## Building pieces

Each building has a grace period after it was last **touched**: built on, repaired, hit, or a door in it opened or closed. Only when the grace period is over does decay start.

| Material | Grace period | Then, from full health to nothing |
|---|---|---|
| Wood | 48 hours | 6 hours |
| Metal | 96 hours | 12 hours |

So a wooden base nobody visits for two days starts crumbling, and a few hours later it's gone.

Two things slow it down:

- **Sheltered walls** (with a building or door both in front and behind within 25 m) take only a fifth of the decay.
- Pieces **holding something up** (a foundation under a wall, a pillar under a ceiling) are spared.

## Placed objects

Boxes, fires, sleeping bags, barricades and the rest decay on their own clock: from full health to nothing in **12 hours** of decay, checked every 5 minutes. They **don't decay at all** when there's a building piece, or a [[item:Wood Shelter]], within 10 m straight above them: the shelter is the only placed item that protects what is under it.

These reset an object's decay:

- opening a storage box
- placing something on it
- a burning camp fire or furnace within 5 m

## In short

Play on your base at least every couple of days, keep your boxes indoors, and a fire burning nearby helps your outdoor gear.
