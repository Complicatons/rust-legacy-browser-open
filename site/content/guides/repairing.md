Guns, tools and armour wear down as you use them. At 0% condition they're broken and stop working.

## The repair bench

[[item:Repair Bench]]: 12 stones, 60 wood, 50 metal fragments, 6 low grade fuel (30 seconds).

Put the item in the bench's slot and press **Repair**. Only items whose wiki page says *Repairable: Yes* can be fixed.

![A worn hatchet in the repair bench](/site/generated/media/guides/repair-bench.jpg)

## What it costs

- You pay **half the item's recipe**, scaled by how worn it is. A gun at 50% costs a quarter of its recipe.
- Every repair also lowers the item's **maximum condition**: it loses 20% of the missing condition plus 5 more points (a gun at 50% comes back at most 85%). A gun repaired again and again ends up with a lower and lower cap.

So repair early and not too often. A badly worn item is cheaper to replace once its cap gets low.
