The [[item:Hunting Bow]] is the first ranged weapon you can make: 35 wood and 5 cloth, no workbench. Its ammo is the [[item:Arrow]]: 4 wood and 1 stone make 4.

## Drawing and firing

1. Hold the fire button. Drawing takes **2 seconds**.
2. Let go to fire. Letting go before the draw is finished drops the arrow back, and you wait **3 seconds** before you can draw again.
3. After firing, you can start the next draw 1 second later, so a bow fires at most once every **3 seconds**.

You can hold a full draw, but not for long. After 1 second your arms start to shake, and after **8 seconds** you give up: the arrow goes back and you wait 3 seconds.

![Holding the draw too long](/site/generated/media/guides/bow-tired.jpg)

## Damage

An arrow does **75 damage** wherever it hits: there's no headshot bonus. It counts as melee damage, so armour's melee protection cuts it (see [Armour](/wiki/guides/armour)).

| Target | Arrows |
|---|---|
| Chicken or rabbit (25) | 1 |
| Stag (80), boar (120), wolf (125) | 2 |
| Mutant wolf (80) | 2 |
| Mutant bear (200) | 3 |
| Bear (250) | 4 |
| Player without armour (100) | 2 |

## Aiming

Arrows fly at **80 m/s** and dip as they go, so aim a little high at long range. An arrow flies for up to 4 seconds. Every arrow you shoot is used up, hit or miss: you can't pick it back up.

The bow is cheap and two arrows bring down most animals, which makes it the best way to hunt early on. See [Animals and hunting](/wiki/guides/animals-and-hunting) for what each animal gives.
