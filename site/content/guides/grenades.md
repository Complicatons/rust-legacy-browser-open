The [[item:F1 Grenade]] costs 80 gunpowder and 40 metal fragments at a workbench, and stacks up to 5.

![An F1 grenade in hand](/site/generated/media/guides/grenade-hand.jpg)

## Throwing

1. Press fire to pull the pin.
2. Hold it for at least **1.25 seconds**, then let go to throw.
3. It leaves your hand at **20 m/s** and goes where you're looking, bouncing off whatever it hits.
4. It explodes **3 seconds** after it leaves your hand.

The fuse starts when you throw, not when you pull the pin, so holding it longer doesn't make it go off sooner.

## The blast

![The blast](/site/generated/media/guides/grenade-blast.jpg)

The explosion reaches **6 m** and does up to **120 damage**, falling off evenly with distance:

| Distance | Damage |
|---|---|
| 0 m | 120 |
| 1.5 m | 90 |
| 3 m | 60 |
| 4.5 m | 30 |
| 6 m or more | 0 |

Anything between you and the blast cuts the damage to **a tenth**: even a thin wall or a rock is good cover. Armour's explosion protection reduces it too (see [Armour](/wiki/guides/armour)).

## Against bases

A grenade does at most 120 to a building piece too, so a wooden wall (1,000 health) takes at least 9. One explosive charge does the same job: see [Raiding](/wiki/guides/raiding).
