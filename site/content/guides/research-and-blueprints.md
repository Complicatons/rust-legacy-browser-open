You can only craft what you know. Every new player already knows **45 blueprints**: the wooden building pieces, doors and storage, cloth armour, the camp fire, furnace and workbench, the hatchet, hunting bow and arrows, the revolver, hand cannon and pipe shotgun with their ammo, bandages, gunpowder, low grade fuel and more (each recipe page says if it is known from the start). Everything else you learn in one of two ways.

## Blueprints

Blueprints only come from two places:

| Where | Blueprints | Chance |
|---|---|---|
| [Weapon Loot Box](/wiki/resources/weapon-loot-box) | P250, Shotgun, 9mm Pistol, F1 Grenade, Large Wood Storage, Flashlight Mod, Laser Sight, Holo Sight, Silencer, Explosive Charge | 40% of rolls give one |
| [Mutant Loot Sack](/wiki/resources/mutant-loot-sack), dropped by every mutant wolf or bear you kill | The same 10, plus the four Kevlar armour pieces | 16.1% of kills give one |

**Airdrops never contain blueprints**, and the box, ammo and medical crates don't either. The Explosive Charge blueprint is the rarest by far (0.1% of weapon box rolls).

Right-click a blueprint and choose **Study**. You learn it at once and the blueprint is used up. If you already know it, you get *"You already have this blueprint"* and keep it.

Every recipe page in the [Crafting](/wiki/recipes) section says how to learn it: known from the start, which loot it drops from and how often, or research.

## Research kit

A [[item:Research Kit 1]] (20 low quality metal, 20 leather, 60 s at a workbench) lets you learn from the item itself:

1. Stand at a [[item:Workbench]].
2. Drag the research kit onto any item that can be researched (its wiki page says *Researchable: Yes*).
3. You learn that item's blueprint straight away.

It always works, there is no chance roll. A research kit has **3 uses**: each research takes one, and **the item you researched is kept**. Researching something you already know does nothing. Research kits come from crafting, [Ammo Loot Boxes](/wiki/resources/ammo-loot-box) (5.5% per roll) and [Mutant Loot Sacks](/wiki/resources/mutant-loot-sack) (5.8% of kills).

## Good first targets

Guns and armour from crates: one [[item:P250]] or [[item:Kevlar Vest]] is enough to make your own forever.
