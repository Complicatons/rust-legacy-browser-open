## Spike walls

| | Recipe | Health |
|---|---|---|
| [[item:Spike Wall]] | 100 wood | 750 |
| [[item:Large Spike Wall]] | 200 wood | 1,500 |

Anyone touching the spikes takes **20 damage a second**, starting the moment they touch it. Hitting a spike wall with a melee weapon from up close hurts the attacker too: they take back a fifth of their hit plus 5.

A spike wall takes half damage from melee (a large one only a quarter) and none from guns. Explosive charges destroy them: 2 for a spike wall, 3 for a large one.

## Barricades and bars

| | Recipe | Health | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[item:Wood Barricade]] | 30 wood | 1,000 | Full melee damage, a quarter less from guns |
| [[item:Metal Window Bars]] | 100 metal fragments (workbench) | 2,000 | Fits in a window; guns and melee can't hurt it |

Window bars let you see and shoot out while nothing gets in: only 4 explosive charges remove them.

## Shelters

A [[item:Wood Shelter]] (50 wood, 1,000 health) is a quick lean-to. Melee only does 10% to it, but it falls to 2 explosive charges.
