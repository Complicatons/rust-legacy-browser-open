Falling only hurts when you hit the ground fast. Dropping off a low wall or rock is fine; jumping off a cliff is not.

## How much it hurts

Fall damage starts when you land at **24 m/s** or more and is certain death at **38 m/s**.

| Landing speed | Damage |
|---|---|
| under 24 m/s | none |
| 24 m/s | 10 |
| 27.5 m/s | 35 (any faster and you bleed) |
| 31 m/s | 60 |
| 34.5 m/s | 85 |
| 38 m/s | 110: dead |

Damage is 10 plus a share of your health that grows from 0 at 24 m/s to all of it at 38 m/s. Above 27.5 m/s you also **bleed** (see [Healing and bleeding](/wiki/guides/healing-and-bleeding)).

## Broken legs

A painful landing can injure your legs. That always happens above about 29 m/s, or when you're already under half health, and otherwise there's a 1-in-3 chance. With injured legs, for 36–44 seconds:

- you're forced to crouch
- you can't jump or sprint
- you move at half speed

The corner of the screen shows **INJURED** until it wears off. A broken leg in the open is often how a fight is lost, so take the long way down.
