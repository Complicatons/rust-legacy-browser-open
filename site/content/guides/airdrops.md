A C130 cargo plane flies over the island and drops **1 to 3 supply crates** on parachutes, a fraction of a second apart. Each crate is full of the best building and raiding loot in the game (though never blueprints: those come from weapon crates and mutants), and everyone nearby will be heading for it.

![The plane](/site/generated/media/guides/airdrop-plane.jpg)

## How drops happen

- **Automatically**: only on busy servers. A drop needs **more than 50 players** online (the default; server owners can change it with `airdrop.min_players`).
- **With a [[item:Supply Signal]]**: throw it and coloured smoke marks the spot. A plane comes for it. The only source of supply signals is an airdrop itself (the 9% bundle below), so they are rare.

![The supply signal's smoke](/site/generated/media/guides/airdrop-smoke.jpg)

Don't stand where the crate lands: getting hit by it kills you.

![The crate coming down](/site/generated/media/guides/airdrop-crate.jpg)

## What's inside

Each crate is one of five bundles:

| Chance | Bundle |
|---|---|
| 29% | 200 gunpowder, 200 metal fragments, 50 wood planks, 5–10 large medkits, 2–5 explosives |
| 21% | 250 9mm ammo, 250 556 ammo, 100 shotgun shells, 10 F1 grenades, 1 explosive charge, 5 large medkits |
| 21% | 2 metal doors, 4 wood foundations, 20 wood pillars, 10 wood walls, 2 wood doorways, 2 explosive charges |
| 21% | metal building parts and 10–15 low quality metal |
| 9% | a supply signal and 10–20 low quality metal |

Even the worst bundle is worth fighting for.
