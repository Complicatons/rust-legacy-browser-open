Animals are your first source of food, cloth, animal fat and blood. Kill one, then hit the body with a hatchet to harvest it.

![A bear](/site/generated/media/guides/animal-bear.jpg)

## The animals

| Animal | Health | Bite | Top speed | Harvest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chicken | 25 | — | 3 m/s | 2 meat, 1 cloth |
| Rabbit | 25 | — | 3 m/s | 1 meat, 2–4 cloth |
| Stag | 80 | — | 8 m/s | 3–5 meat, 8–10 cloth, 3 fat |
| Boar | 120 | — | 4.5 m/s | 3–5 meat, 4–6 cloth, 8–10 fat |
| Wolf | 125 | **15** every 1.5 s | 8 m/s | 3–5 meat, 20 cloth, 1–3 blood, 4–5 fat |
| Bear | 250 | **30** every 1.5 s | 5 m/s | 5–10 meat, 8 leather, 10 cloth, 6 blood, 10 fat |
| Mutant wolf | 80 | **25** every 1 s | 8 m/s | 1–2 meat, 1–2 cloth |
| Mutant bear | 200 | **30–45** every 1.5 s | 5 m/s | 1–2 meat, 1–3 leather |

"Meat" is [[item:Raw Chicken Breast]]: every animal gives the same meat. Cook it before eating (see [Food and cooking](/wiki/guides/food-and-cooking)).

Wolves and stags both run at 8 m/s, so you can't outrun a wolf. Bears are slower, but have twice a wolf's health.

![A wolf](/site/generated/media/guides/animal-wolf.jpg)

## Mutants

Mutant wolves and bears only spawn in 16 zones around the radioactive compounds. They bite harder than the normal ones: a mutant wolf bites for 25 every second (a wolf: 15 every 1.5 s), a mutant bear for 30–45 (a bear: 30). Their bodies give almost nothing to harvest, **but every kill drops a [Mutant Loot Sack](/wiki/resources/mutant-loot-sack)** beside the body.

![A mutant wolf charging](/site/generated/media/guides/animal-mutant-wolf.jpg)

![The loot sack a mutant wolf left beside its body](/site/generated/media/guides/mutant-sack.jpg)

The sack holds one roll from its own loot list: mostly ammo, food, gunpowder and junk, but also a chance of something good:

| In the sack | Chance per kill |
|---|---|
| A blueprint: P250, Shotgun, 9mm Pistol, F1 Grenade, Large Wood Storage, Flashlight Mod, Laser Sight, Holo Sight, Silencer or (very rarely) Explosive Charge | 14.2% |
| A [[item:Research Kit 1]] | 5.8% |
| A Kevlar armour blueprint (helmet, vest, pants or boots) | 1.9% |
| A piece of leather armour | 9.4% |

The sack disappears **5 minutes** after the kill, or as soon as it's emptied, so loot it straight away. Turn on *Mutants* on the [Island map](/island) to see where they live.

## Where animals live

Normal animals spawn in 16 wildlife zones across the island (map overlay *Animals*). The [Animals](/wiki/animals) section has a page for each one.
