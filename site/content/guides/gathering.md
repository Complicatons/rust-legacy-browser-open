Everything you build starts here. What you get per hit depends on the tool and what you hit.

![Mining an ore node with a pick axe](/site/generated/media/guides/gather-ore.jpg)

## Resources per hit

| Tool | Tree (wood) | Wood pile | Ore node | Bear body | Swing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[item:Rock]] | 0.75 | 7 | 2 | 3 | 1.95 s |
| [[item:Stone Hatchet]] | 0.75 | 7 | 2 | 3 | 0.9 s |
| [[item:Hatchet]] | 1 | 10 | 3 | 3 | 0.9 s |
| [[item:Pick Axe]] | 1.5 | 20 | 6 | 1 | 2 s |

Trees give fractions that add up over several hits (a rock gives 3 wood every 4 hits). Wood piles and ore nodes drop the fraction each hit.

What that means:

- **Wood**: the pick axe gets the most per hit from wood piles, but the hatchet swings more than twice as often. Both fill your bag fast.
- **Ore**: the pick axe is best, at 6 per hit.
- **Harvesting animals**: use a hatchet. Bodies give 3 per hit for bears, 2 for boars, stags and wolves, and 1 for chickens and rabbits. The pick axe gets 1 per hit from all of those except chickens and rabbits, where it gets nothing at all (its half-point rounds down to zero every hit).

![Chopping a tree](/site/generated/media/guides/gather-tree.jpg)

## Node types

| Node | Gives |
|---|---|
| Wood pile | 60–100 wood |
| Ore 1 (sulfur rich) | 8–10 sulfur ore, 2–5 metal ore, 3–4 stones |
| Ore 2 (metal rich) | 1–4 sulfur ore, 8–10 metal ore, 3–4 stones |
| Ore 3 (stone rich) | 1–3 sulfur ore, 1–3 metal ore, 10–15 stones |

Each hit takes a random resource from the node until it's empty. Then the node disappears, and a new one spawns somewhere in its zone later. Turn on *Resources* on the [Island map](/island) to see the spawn zones.

Trees run out too: hit the same spot long enough and you'll see *"There's no wood left here"*.
