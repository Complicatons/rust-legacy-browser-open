## Text chat

Press **Enter** or **T** to type, then **Enter** to send.

| Rule | Limit |
|---|---|
| Message length | 128 characters |
| How often | 1 message a second |
| How long lines stay on screen | 16 seconds |

![Typing in chat](/site/generated/media/guides/chat-open.jpg)

## Voice

Hold **V** to talk. Your microphone is only used while the key is down, never when the page loads. Players near you hear you, fading out to silence at **100 m**, so voice is local: shout across a field, not across the island.

## The console

**F1** opens the original console window. Commands the game doesn't handle on your side are sent to the server, and its replies show in the console. Server admins use it for their commands.

All keys can be changed in Options; see the full list in [Controls](/wiki/guides/controls).
