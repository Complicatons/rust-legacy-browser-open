Guns and melee can't get through walls (see [Building basics](/wiki/guides/building-basics)). To raid a base you need explosives.

## The explosive charge

[[item:Explosive Charge]] (C4): stick it on a door or wall like placing any item. It ticks for **5 seconds**, then explodes:

- The piece it's stuck to takes the full **600 damage**.
- Anything else within **3 m** takes less the further away it is, and only a tenth if something is in the way.

Making one is a big job (691 sulfur, see [Gunpowder and explosives](/wiki/guides/gunpowder-and-explosives)).

## Charges needed

| Target | Health | Charges |
|---|---|---|
| [[item:Wooden Door]] | 500 | **1** |
| [[item:Metal Door]] | 1,000 | **2** |
| [[item:Wood Wall]], doorway or window | 1,000 | **2** |
| [[item:Wood Gate]] | 1,800 | **3** |
| [[item:Metal Wall]], doorway or window | 2,000 | **4** |
| [[item:Metal Window Bars]] | 2,000 | **4** |
| [[item:Wood Gateway]] | 2,000 | **5** |
| Foundations, pillars, ceilings | — | **can't be destroyed** |

The Wood Gateway takes 5 rather than 4 because it shrugs off a quarter of every blast. The [Raiding table](/wiki/raiding) lists every piece.

So the cheapest way in is almost always the door: one charge for a wooden door, two for metal.

## When a piece goes

When a wall or other piece is destroyed, anything resting on it goes with it. There's no slow collapse; it just disappears.

![The door gone after the blast](/site/generated/media/guides/raid-after.jpg)

## Grenades

An [[item:F1 Grenade]] (80 gunpowder, 40 metal fragments) explodes for **120 damage** across **6 m**, losing strength with distance. It's a weapon against players far more than a raiding tool: it would take at least 9 to break a wooden wall.

## Defending

- Metal doors double the cost of getting in.
- More walls between the outside and your loot means more charges.
- Spike walls and barricades slow raiders down (see [Defences](/wiki/guides/defences)).
