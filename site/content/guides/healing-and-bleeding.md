## Bleeding

Bullets and melee hits that take more than 5% of your health can start **bleeding**, and the bigger the hit, the more likely. While you bleed you lose health every 8 seconds and **you don't heal on your own**. A hard fall can make you bleed too.

![Bleeding after a bad fall: the red BLEEDING sign in the corner](/site/generated/media/guides/fall-injured.jpg)

## Medical items

| Item | Heals | Stops bleeding | Recipe |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[item:Bandage]] | — | Yes | 2 cloth, 10 s |
| [[item:Small Medkit]] | 40 | Yes | 2 cloth, 2 blood, 10 s |
| [[item:Large Medkit]] | 100 | Yes | 3 cloth, 3 blood, 10 s, workbench |

Medical items only work when you're hurt, and you can use one every 5 seconds. Their healing comes in gradually. Taking a bullet, melee or explosion hit stops any healing still in progress.

## Blood

[[item:Blood]] for medkits comes from wolves (1–3) and bears (6), or from a [[item:Blood Draw Kit]]: each use takes 25 of your own health for 1 blood. The kit isn't used up, but it refuses when you have 25 health or less.

## Healing for free

With no bleeding, under 500 rads, not cold and no damage for 10 seconds, you heal 10 health a minute on your own, paid for with calories. Stand next to a burning fire and it's five times faster.
