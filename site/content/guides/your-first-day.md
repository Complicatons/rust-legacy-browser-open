You wake up on the island with almost nothing. The first hour decides whether your first night is warm or short. This is the order that works.

## 1. Gather wood with the rock

Look for a **wood pile** (a stack of logs on the ground) and hit it with your [[item:Rock]]. Each hit gives about 7 wood, and a pile holds 60–100. Trees work too, but slower: a rock gets you 3 wood every 4 hits.

![Hitting a wood pile with the rock](/site/generated/media/guides/gather-woodpile.jpg)

## 2. Make a stone hatchet

Rocks on the ground are ore nodes. Hitting them gives [[item:Stones]] plus some ore. With 5 stones and 10 wood, craft a [[item:Stone Hatchet]] (20 seconds). It swings twice as fast as the rock and is how you'll gather wood from now on.

## 3. A fire before night

Nights are nearly black and cold. A [[item:Camp Fire]] costs just 5 wood. Light it (hold **E** on it and pick *Ignite*) and feed it wood. Standing near a burning fire keeps you warm, and it heals you five times faster.

## 4. Hunt for cloth

Animals give meat and [[item:Cloth]]. A [[item:Sleeping Bag]] needs 15 cloth, and with one placed you respawn next to it instead of at a random spawn point (there are 63 across the island).

## 5. Shelter

A [[item:Wood Shelter]] (50 wood) is the quickest roof. When you have more wood, build a real hut (see [Building basics](/wiki/guides/building-basics)) and put a [[item:Wooden Door]] on it. Doors come with a 4-digit code lock.
