A raid is a big investment: one explosive charge takes 691 sulfur. Planning decides whether it pays off. The numbers behind each step are in [Raiding](/wiki/guides/raiding).

## 1. Scout the base

Walk around it before you spend anything. Count:

- **Doors** you'd have to pass, and whether each is wood or metal.
- **Walls**: wood or metal. Doorways and windows count as walls.
- **Window bars**: as strong as a metal wall.

Foundations, pillars and ceilings can't be destroyed, so the only ways in are through doors, gates, walls, doorways and windows.

## 2. Find the cheapest way in

Add up the explosive charges along every route to the loot and take the cheapest:

| Piece | Charges |
|---|---|
| Wooden door | 1 |
| Metal door | 2 |
| Wood wall, doorway or window | 2 |
| Wood gate | 3 |
| Metal wall, doorway or window | 4 |
| Metal window bars | 4 |
| Wood gateway | 5 |

A metal door followed by a wooden door costs 3 charges, and going through a metal wall instead costs 4. The door is usually the answer, unless the builder stacked several of them.

## 3. Build the raid kit

| Charges | Sulfur | Charcoal | Metal fragments | Low grade fuel | Leather |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 691 | 616 | 158 | 30 | 5 |
| 2 | 1,382 | 1,232 | 316 | 60 | 10 |
| 3 | 2,073 | 1,848 | 474 | 90 | 15 |
| 4 | 2,764 | 2,464 | 632 | 120 | 20 |

What that means in gathering, per charge:

- about **200 sulfur ore** (3–4 sulfur each);
- about **250 wood** burned in a furnace for the charcoal (a furnace leaves 5 charcoal for every 2 wood);
- about **30 metal ore** for the fragments;
- **60 animal fat** and 30 cloth for the low grade fuel, and 5 leather.

Charcoal is the slow part: one furnace makes 10 a minute, so a charge's worth takes about an hour of burning. Run several fires at once. Explosives and the charge need a [[item:Workbench]], where crafting is three times faster. The full chain is in [Gunpowder and explosives](/wiki/guides/gunpowder-and-explosives).

## 4. Pick your moment

- **Players log off where they stand.** Their sleeping body stays where they logged off, usually inside their base, and it can be killed and looted like anyone else (see [Death and respawning](/wiki/guides/death-and-respawning)).
- **Abandoned bases decay.** A wood base nobody has touched for 48 hours (metal: 96) starts losing health, and a weakened piece needs fewer charges. See [Decay](/wiki/guides/decay).
- **Raids take time.** Crafting and placing charges, then walking through the base, leaves you exposed: bring a gun and someone to watch your back.

## 5. Place the charges

![Placing a charge on a wooden door](/site/generated/media/guides/raid-aim.jpg)

Hold the charge and place it on the door or wall like any other item. It goes off **5 seconds** later. Step back: everything within 3 m takes part of the blast, you included.

Two charges on the same piece add up, so for a metal door place both, then step back. When a piece is destroyed, anything resting on it goes too.

![After the blast](/site/generated/media/guides/raid-after.jpg)

## Without explosives

- **Wooden doors** take a tenth of melee damage: about **200 hatchet hits**, three minutes of swinging, 1,000 calories and more than one hatchet's life. Metal doors can't be hurt by melee at all.
- **Guessing the code** costs 5 seconds per wrong try. All 10,000 codes would take almost 14 hours.
- Guns can't damage doors or walls.

## After the raid

Take what you can carry and go: the owner may be on the way back to a sleeping bag inside. Then think about your own base: see [Defending your base](/wiki/guides/defending-your-base).
