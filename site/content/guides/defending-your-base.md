No base is raid-proof, but every layer costs a raider 691 sulfur per explosive charge. The goal is to make getting to your loot cost more than it's worth.

## Know what can be broken

- **Foundations, pillars and ceilings can never be destroyed.** Nobody comes up through the floor or down through the roof.
- **Guns can't damage** walls, doors or window bars at all.
- **Melee** can't hurt walls or metal doors, and a wooden door takes only a tenth of it.
- **Explosive charges** are the real threat. See [Raiding](/wiki/guides/raiding) for how many each piece takes.

So a raid always comes through a door, gate, wall, doorway or window.

## Upgrade what matters first

| Change | Your cost | Raider's extra cost |
|---|---|---|
| Wooden door → metal door | 200 fragments | +1 charge (691 sulfur) |
| Wood wall → metal wall | 60 fragments | +2 charges (1,382 sulfur) |
| Open window → metal window bars | 100 fragments | 4 charges |

A metal wall costs a third of a metal door and adds twice as many charges, but a raider will always pick your weakest piece. Upgrade the cheapest way in first: usually the door.

## Layers

Every door or wall a raider has to pass adds its charges again:

| Way to the loot | Charges |
|---|---|
| One wooden door | 1 |
| One metal door | 2 |
| Metal door, then another metal door | 4 |
| Metal door, then two more metal doors | 6 |
| Through a metal wall into a room with metal walls | 8 |

Keep your loot in an inner room, behind more than one door, and make sure no outside wall of that room is weaker than the doors in front of it.

## Keep boxes away from the blast

A charge also hurts everything within **3 m**, so don't put boxes right behind a door or an outside wall. Spread loot over several boxes in different rooms: a raider who breaks into one room doesn't get it all.

## Outside the walls

- **[[item:Metal Window Bars]]** let you see and shoot out of a window and take 4 charges to remove.
- **Spike walls** hurt anyone who touches them (20 a second). They don't stop explosives, but they slow raiders down. See [Defences](/wiki/guides/defences).

## Don't let it decay

A wood base nobody touches for **48 hours** (metal: 96) starts losing health, and a weaker wall needs fewer charges. Walls with buildings close on both sides decay at a fifth of the rate. Log in, open a door, repair. See [Decay](/wiki/guides/decay).

## Log off inside

Your body stays in the world when you log off. Do it inside a locked room, ideally behind your strongest doors, with a [[item:Sleeping Bag]] or [[item:Bed]] inside so you can come back after a death. Change your door codes if you've shared them with someone you no longer trust: *Change Lock* takes everyone else's access away (see [Doors and code locks](/wiki/guides/doors-and-locks)).
