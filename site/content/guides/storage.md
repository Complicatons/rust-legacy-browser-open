Anything in your pockets drops when you die. Anything in a box stays, as long as nobody breaks in.

| Box | Recipe | Slots | Health |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[item:Small Stash]] | 10 leather | 3 | 100 |
| [[item:Wood Storage Box]] | 30 wood | 12 | 500 |
| [[item:Large Wood Storage]] | 60 wood (workbench) | 36 | 1,000 |

Hold **E** on a box and choose *Open* to move items between it and your inventory.

![An open wood storage box](/site/generated/media/guides/storage.jpg)

## Tips

- Boxes can be destroyed, and everything inside goes with it. Keep them inside a locked base.
- Opening a box resets its [decay](/wiki/guides/decay), and a box under a roof doesn't decay at all.
- A small stash is easy to hide but fragile at 100 health.
