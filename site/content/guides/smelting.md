Ore is useless until it's smelted, and only a [[item:Furnace]] is hot enough. A camp fire isn't.

## Build a furnace

[[item:Furnace]]: 15 stones, 20 wood, 10 [[item:Low Grade Fuel]], 30 seconds. Low grade fuel is 2 [[item:Animal Fat]] and 1 [[item:Cloth]] (2 seconds), so hunt a boar or bear first.

![A lit furnace](/site/generated/media/guides/furnace.jpg)

## How it works

Put wood in as fuel and ore in the 3 smelting slots, then hold **E** and choose *Ignite*.

- It burns **2 wood every 30 seconds**.
- Each time, it smelts **up to 2** of every ore in its smelting slots.
- [[item:Metal Ore]] gives **3–8** [[item:Metal Fragments]] each.
- [[item:Sulfur Ore]] gives **3–4** [[item:Sulfur]] each.
- Burning wood leaves [[item:Charcoal]], which you need for gunpowder.

So one slot of ore smelts 4 ore a minute, and three slots 12. It goes out when the wood runs out or the output fills up.

Don't cook meat in a furnace: it's hotter than a camp fire and the meat burns to charcoal. A furnace does turn [[item:Cloth]] into [[item:Leather]], one for one.

## Metal for building

[[item:Low Quality Metal]] is 15 metal fragments, made at a workbench in 2 seconds. Metal walls, guns and armour all use it.
