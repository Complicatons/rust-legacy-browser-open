Food refills your calories (see [Health, food and rads](/wiki/guides/vitals-and-hud)). You can eat once every 3 seconds, and calories above 3,000 are wasted.

## What food gives

| Food | Calories | Heals | Anti-rads | Water |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[item:Small Rations]] | 750 | 25 | 300 | 6 L |
| [[item:Cooked Chicken Breast]] | 500 | 2 | — | — |
| [[item:Chocolate Bar]] | 350 | 8 | — | — |
| [[item:Can of Beans]] | 300 | 5 | 50 | 0.2 L |
| [[item:Can of Tuna]] | 200 | 5 | 25 | 0.25 L |
| [[item:Granola Bar]] | 150 | 5 | — | — |
| [[item:Small Water Bottle]] | — | 2 | 75 | 1 L |
| [[item:Raw Chicken Breast]] | 80 | −10 | — | — |

Healing from food happens slowly over the next seconds, not all at once.

## Don't eat it raw

[[item:Raw Chicken Breast]] takes 10 health and poisons you. While poisoned you throw up every 10 seconds, losing 100–200 calories and 2 health each time. Cook it first: cooked chicken gives 500 calories.

## Cooking on a camp fire

Open a lit [[item:Camp Fire]] (hold **E**, *Open*) and put meat in the cooking slots, with wood as fuel.

![A camp fire burning at night](/site/generated/media/guides/campfire.jpg)

- A camp fire burns **1 wood every 60 seconds**.
- Each time it burns wood it cooks **up to 2** of every item in its 3 cooking slots.
- Burning wood leaves [[item:Charcoal]] behind, which you need for gunpowder.
- It goes out when it runs out of wood or when its output is full.

A [[item:Furnace]] (2 wood every 30 seconds) is the only fire hot enough to smelt ore (see [Smelting](/wiki/guides/smelting)), but it's too hot for food: meat put in a furnace burns to charcoal. Cook on a camp fire.

## Bonus: a furnace turns cloth into leather

In the original game data, [[item:Cloth]] in a furnace turns into [[item:Leather]] one for one. It won't go on a camp fire: a camp fire's cooking slots only take raw food. Otherwise leather mostly comes from bears.
