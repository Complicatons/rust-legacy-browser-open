The island is big, so it pays to know how fast you can cross it and when to slow down.

![Morning on the meadow](/site/generated/media/guides/move-meadow.jpg)

## Speeds

| How | Speed |
|---|---|
| Walking forward | **4 m/s** |
| Walking sideways | 4 m/s |
| Walking backwards | 3 m/s |
| Sprinting forward | **8 m/s** |
| Sprinting sideways | 4.8 m/s |
| Crouching | **2.2 m/s** (55% of walking) |

Sprint speed builds up over the first **0.4 seconds**, and so does the slowdown when you crouch. You can't sprint while crouching or aiming down the sights.

At a sprint, crossing a kilometre takes about two minutes. Wolves and stags also run at 8 m/s, so you can't outrun a wolf (see [Animals and hunting](/wiki/guides/animals-and-hunting)).

## Crouching

Hold **Left Ctrl**. Your view drops by about half a metre, which is enough to hide behind a low rock. Crouching also steadies a gun:

- **20% less recoil** crouched, with every gun except the pipe shotgun.
- Aiming and crouching together: most guns get the aiming reduction plus another 10%. An aimed, crouched M4 kicks **60% less**.
- Shotguns get one or the other, never both.

You stay crouched under a low ceiling until there's room to stand.

## Jumping

A jump lifts you about **1.5 m** (a very quick tap gives 1 m). You can jump once a second, and only from the ground.

## Slopes, steps and drops

- You walk up steps and ledges up to **0.5 m** high without jumping.
- Uphill slows you down, more the steeper it gets, and slopes steeper than **65°** stop you.
- Gravity is strong: a straight drop of about **8 m** starts to hurt, and about **21 m** is deadly. See [Fall damage](/wiki/guides/fall-damage).

## What slows you down

- **Injured legs** after a bad fall: forced to crouch, no jumping or sprinting, half speed for 36–44 seconds.
- **Starving**: you still move, but every melee swing takes twice as long (see [Melee weapons and tools](/wiki/guides/melee-weapons)).
