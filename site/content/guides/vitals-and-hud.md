The panel in the bottom-right corner shows **Health**, **Food** and **Rads**.

## Health

You have 100 health. You heal slowly on your own when all of these are true:

- you've taken no damage for 10 seconds
- you aren't bleeding
- your rads are under 500
- you aren't cold

Healing runs at 10 health a minute and costs 5 calories per health point. Near a burning fire or furnace it's **five times faster**.

## Food (calories)

| | Calories |
|---|---|
| You start with | 1,250 |
| Most you can hold | 3,000 |
| Burned per hour standing still | 300 |
| Burned per hour at full activity | 3,000 |
| Extra burned per hour when cold at night | 1,500 |

When calories hit zero you starve and lose 10 health a minute. Water is tracked but never runs out. Drinking only matters for the radiation some bottles remove.

## Rads

Radiation builds up in radiation zones and wears off on its own over time. Above 500 it starts hurting you. See [Radiation](/wiki/guides/radiation).
