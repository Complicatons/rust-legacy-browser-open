## The chain

1. **[[item:Charcoal]]**: left behind by every wood a camp fire or furnace burns.
2. **[[item:Sulfur]]**: smelt [[item:Sulfur Ore]] in a furnace (see [Smelting](/wiki/guides/smelting)).
3. **[[item:Gunpowder]]**: 2 charcoal + 2 sulfur.
4. **[[item:Explosives]]**: 20 gunpowder, 2 low grade fuel, 5 sulfur, 10 metal fragments (workbench).
5. **[[item:Explosive Charge]]**: 15 explosives, 1 flare, 5 leather (workbench, 2 minutes).

## What one explosive charge really costs

| Resource | Amount |
|---|---|
| Sulfur | 691 |
| Charcoal | 616 |
| Metal fragments | 158 |
| Low grade fuel | 30 |
| Leather | 5 |

That includes the flare (8 gunpowder, 8 metal fragments). It's why a charge is a big deal: see [Raiding](/wiki/guides/raiding) for how many you need.
