Every player has **100 health**. Fights are won by landing hits first, so it helps to know how many it takes.

![Aiming the M4 with a holo sight](/site/generated/media/guides/weapon-holo.jpg)

## Shots to kill

| Weapon | No armour: body | No armour: head | Full Kevlar: body | Full Kevlar: head |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[item:Bolt Action Rifle]] | 2 | **1** | 3 | **1** |
| [[item:M4]] | 3–4 | 1–2 | 5–6 | 2 |
| [[item:P250]] | 3–4 | 1–2 | 5–6 | 2 |
| [[item:MP5A4]] | 4 | 2 | 7 | 3 |
| [[item:9mm Pistol]] | 4 | 2 | 7 | 3 |
| [[item:Hunting Bow]] | 2 | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| [[item:Hatchet]] | 4 | 4 | 7 | 7 |

Gun damage varies a little from shot to shot, so some need one more hit than others. Only bullets get the **3× headshot** bonus: arrows and melee hit just as hard anywhere. A silencer cuts gun damage to 60%.

## Armour

Full Kevlar takes **85** off bullet and melee damage out of 200, so you take 57.5% of every hit. Even a cloth set (33) cuts hits by a sixth. See [Armour](/wiki/guides/armour) for every piece.

## Aim steady

- **Aim down the sights** (right mouse): rifles, the MP5 and shotguns lose half their recoil, the P250 and 9mm pistol 35%, the revolver a quarter.
- **Crouch** as well: another 10% off.
- You can't sprint while aiming. See [Moving around](/wiki/guides/moving-around).

## Bleeding and healing

Hits that take more than 5% of your health can make you **bleed**: you lose health every 8 seconds and don't heal on your own. A [[item:Bandage]] stops it.

- [[item:Small Medkit]] heals 40, [[item:Large Medkit]] 100, gradually. Both stop bleeding.
- You can use a medical item every 5 seconds.
- Any bullet, melee or explosion hit stops the healing still in progress, so heal behind cover.

More in [Healing and bleeding](/wiki/guides/healing-and-bleeding).

## Grenades and high ground

- An [[item:F1 Grenade]] does up to 120 within 6 m, and a tenth of that through cover. See [Grenades](/wiki/guides/grenades).
- Falls hurt from about 8 m, so don't jump off the roof you're fighting from. See [Fall damage](/wiki/guides/fall-damage).

## Before a fight

- Eat: starving doubles the time between melee swings.
- Keep your rads under 500, or you stop healing.
- Carry bandages in your belt (keys 1–6) so you can reach them fast.
