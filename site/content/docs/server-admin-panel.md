**Server admin** (`/admin`) is where a server's team runs its players. Open it from [Your servers](/owner), or from the account menu if you're on a server team. If you're on more than one server, pick one at the top.

## Sections

| Section | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| Live players | Who's on now, from the browser and the desktop game, with each one's anti-cheat score. Highlighted rows have reached the review score (50). |
| Action | Kick or ban, and in-game actions through the server plugin: message, teleport to, bring, kick or ban a desktop player. |
| Evidence | A player's flags, each with what was seen. Movement and shots come with a plot. |
| Screenshots | Ask for a screenshot of a player's screen, or let serious flags take one automatically. |
| Linked-account holds | New accounts that share a device or login with a banned account wait here until you decide. |
| Item transfers | Items passed between players, to spot traders for cheaters. |
| Join policy | Who may join: minimum account age, Steam linked, verified phone, and Steam profile checks. |
| Reports | Player reports from `/report`, to resolve with a note. |
| Bans | Every ban, with who made it and why. Unban from here. |
| Server team | Who has a role on this server. |
| Audit log | Every action taken by anyone on the team. |

Scores fade over time. A flag is evidence for a person to judge, not proof on its own.
