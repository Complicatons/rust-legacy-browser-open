## Report a player

In game, type:

```
/report <player name> <what happened>
```

The report goes to that server's team on this website with your account attached, so it can't be sent anonymously. Desktop characters must be [linked](/docs/link-a-desktop-character) first. You can send up to 10 reports an hour.

## What the server team sees

Every server runs our anti-cheat on browser players. It scores impossible movement, shots and actions, and keeps the evidence. A high score is a reason for a person to look, not an automatic ban. The team decides, and every kick and ban is in their audit log.

## Bans

- **Server bans** are made by a server's own team and apply to that server only. They can be timed or permanent.
- **Network bans** are made by the Rust Legacy team for cheating or abuse across servers, and apply on every server.
- Making a new account to dodge a ban doesn't work: accounts that share devices or logins with a banned account are held for review.

To appeal a server ban, ask that server's owner. For a network ban, open a ticket in our [Discord](https://discord.gg/your-invite).
