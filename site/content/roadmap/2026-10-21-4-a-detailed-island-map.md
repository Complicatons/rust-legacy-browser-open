The [island map](/island) has three layers. **Aerial** is a picture of our port taken from high above. **Modern** is our own map, drawn straight from the game files. Until now Modern was a simple sketch: coloured ground, dots for trees and plain shapes for buildings. Zoomed in, the Aerial showed much more.

The Modern map is now drawn in much more detail, so zooming in is worth it.

## What's on it now

- **Rocks and cliffs.** Every one of the island's 5,278 rock formations is drawn from its real shape and texture, with its shadow. Cliffs and rock fields look the way they do in the game. The Aerial can't show them, because the game hides rocks that far from the camera.
- **Buildings and props.** Warehouses, sheds, walls, fences, barrels, containers and pallets are drawn from their real models and textures, roofs included, with outlines and shadows.
- **Trees.** All 4,302 trees and bushes have their real leafy outlines, each with a soft shadow.
- **Roads.** The dirt road uses its own texture, with its worn edges.
- **Ground.** The ground uses the game's terrain colours and textures, with hills shaded so slopes and ridges stand out.
- **Sea.** Shallow water shows the sea bed, then gets deeper and darker, with surf along the shore.

## Same map, more detail

The map is now twice as sharp (half a metre per pixel instead of one) and has one more zoom level. Crate spots, radiation, spawn zones and contour lines still sit on top, in the same places. Turn on **Compare** to slide between Modern and Aerial. The whole map is about 15 MB and loads one piece at a time as you move around.

## Hover to see what's there

Move the mouse over the map and a small label says what is under it: **Loot crate** with the odds of each crate type, a radiation, mutant, wildlife, resource or no-build zone with what it holds, or a place with what's there and its radiation. Anywhere near a named place, the label also gives the area's name.

## The classic map

The island page now has the classic community map that players used back in the day. It's lined up using the map's own X and Z grid, so every crate spot, zone and place marker sits on the right spot, and **Compare** slides between it and the Modern or Aerial map. The community map has the island turned a quarter turn from ours, so its writing reads sideways.
