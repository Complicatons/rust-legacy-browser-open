In the original game every mutant wolf and mutant bear leaves a loot sack when it dies. Our port didn't: offline, a dead mutant dropped nothing. It does now.

## Like the original

The sack lands 1.5 m from the body in a random direction, settles on the ground's slope, and fills itself with one roll from the mutants' own loot list. That can be ammo, food or junk, but also a blueprint, a research kit or leather armour. Walk up to it and press **E** to search it. It disappears 5 minutes after the kill, or as soon as it's empty. Normal wolves and bears still leave nothing. Online, the server drops the sack and the browser draws it.
