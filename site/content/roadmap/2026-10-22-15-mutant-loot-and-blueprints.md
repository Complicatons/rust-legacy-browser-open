Going over the wiki against the original game data turned up things it was missing, and a few guides that had them wrong.

## Mutants drop loot

Every mutant wolf and mutant bear leaves a **[Mutant Loot Sack](/wiki/resources/mutant-loot-sack)** beside its body. It holds one roll from its own loot list: mostly ammo, food and junk, but 16.1% of kills give a blueprint, 5.8% a research kit, and some give leather armour. The sack disappears after 5 minutes. The guides used to say mutants give almost nothing; that was wrong.

## Where blueprints come from

Blueprints only come from **Weapon Loot Boxes** and **mutant loot sacks**, never from airdrops or the other crates. Every recipe page now says how you learn it: known from the start (45 are), which loot it drops from and how often, or research with a research kit.

## More from the game's own data

- The **Supply Crate** from airdrops has its own page with everything it can hold.
- Animal pages show how hard and how often each animal bites: a mutant wolf bites for 25 every second, a normal wolf for 15 every 1.5 seconds.
- 37 items show their original in-game description.
- Guns show how far a bullet goes, how far a shot is heard, recoil and pellets. Tools show what one hit gathers.

## More corrections

- **Airdrops drop 1 to 3 crates**, not one: the plane lets them go a fraction of a second apart.
- **Metal building** now has its own table: twice the health of wood for walls and foundations. Nobody knows it from the start and its blueprints never drop, so you research a metal piece taken from an airdrop.
- **The handmade lockpick** opens a locked death sack (on servers that lock them), every time. It never works on doors.
- **A wood shelter** keeps the things under it from decaying, just like a building does.
