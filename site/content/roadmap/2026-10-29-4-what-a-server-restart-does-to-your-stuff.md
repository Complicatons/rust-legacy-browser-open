A server has to restart sometimes — for an update, or just to free up memory. We checked what that does to a player who was offline (asleep) at the time.

## What we tested

Two test players gathered what they could, then logged off to become sleepers. We stopped the real server, started it again, and logged them back in to see what came back.

## What we found

Both woke up exactly where they'd fallen asleep, with everything they'd gathered — ore, stone and their starting gear alike. Health and hunger carried over correctly too. A restart costs a player nothing.
