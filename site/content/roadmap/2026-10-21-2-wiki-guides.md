The [wiki](/wiki) already listed every item. It now also explains how the game plays, the way the old fandom wiki did. The new [Guides](/wiki/guides) section has 28 guides in six groups: Start here, Survival, Gathering & crafting, Building, Combat & raiding and The world.

## What the guides cover

- **Start here:** your first day, the vitals and HUD, the inventory and crafting, research and blueprints.
- **Survival:** food and cooking, healing and bleeding, radiation, cold and night, dying and respawning, animals and hunting.
- **Gathering & crafting:** what each tool gets from trees, rocks and animals, smelting, gunpowder and explosives, loot crates, repairing.
- **Building:** the basics, doors and locks, decay, storage, defences.
- **Combat & raiding:** weapons and ammo, armour, weapon mods, raiding, airdrops.
- **The world:** the island, fall damage, chat, voice and the console.

Every number comes from the original game's data and code, not from memory: how much radiation hurts, how fast you fall before it breaks your legs, how much a charge does to a door, how often each airdrop bundle appears. Item names in a guide link to their wiki pages, and places link to the [island map](/island).

## Pictures

Each guide has pictures taken in our port with the normal game, not mock-ups. A new capture tool sets up each scene in the game and takes the shots, so they can be taken again whenever the game changes.

## Loot crates

Crate pages now show how many item groups a crate rolls, worked out the way the original does: the count is capped by the number of groups in the crate's list, so a box with two groups never rolls three. Box loot rolls 1–2, ammo and weapon boxes 1, medical boxes 1–2. Lists where the original hands out one of everything now show each of those items as certain instead of splitting the chance between them.
