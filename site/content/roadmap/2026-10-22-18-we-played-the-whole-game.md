We played the browser game the way a new player would. We started at the menu, went through the options and the server browser, and spawned on the island. Then we gathered, hunted, cooked, built a base, fought, died, respawned at our sleeping bag, and blew the door off our own base with an Explosive Charge. We played offline, and online with two players on our test server: chat, the console, an airdrop, and leaving and joining again.

Most of it already matched the original. Seven things didn't, and all seven are fixed.

## Number keys work like the original's

- A new character starts with **empty hands**. Press 1 to take out the rock, and press 1 again to put it away.
- Pressing the number of something you can't hold now **uses** it, and you keep holding what you had. Food gets eaten, a blueprint gets studied, and a lockpick gives its message. Before, the number key swapped your rock for that item.
- You can't switch while your weapon is still recovering from a shot or swing, or while one of your arrows is in the air.

## Repair walls and doors with wood

Put Wood on your belt, look at a damaged wooden wall, door or box, and press its number. Each press uses one Wood and heals a share of the health back, at most once every 2 seconds. Metal pieces take metal fragments, and sleeping bags and beds take cloth. You can't repair something that was damaged in the last 5 seconds. This worked on real servers already; now it works offline too.

## Smaller fixes

- **Workbench and fire range:** you count as at a workbench, or warm by a fire, half a metre further away. The original measures from a small sphere around the player, not from the feet.
- **Crafting bar:** the empty progress bar stays visible while a recipe is picked, as in the original.
- **`suicide` offline:** typing it in the F1 console now kills you, with the original message "You killed yourself. You silly sod."
- **Fire text:** pointing at a fire says "Use", as in the original. This one was fixed the same day in another update.

## Things that look odd but match the original

- Pressing E on a burning fire puts it out.
- Releasing the bow before it's fully drawn cancels the shot.
- A fall shows the red damage mark on the right.
- Stairs can't go in a room that already has a ceiling.
