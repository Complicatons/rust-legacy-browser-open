This page is new. It tells the story of the port from the first build on 16 October, with the pictures and clips of each step, and ends with what is still to come.

From now on, every finished piece of work gets its own entry here when it is published, so this page stays the place to see where the project is. Each new entry is also posted to #updates on the Discord, and shorter news still goes to [Updates](/updates).

## Progress at the top

The roadmap now opens with a progress bar showing how far the port has come overall, plus a smaller bar for each area: the world, survival, building, combat, menus, online play, the first real server, the website, anti-cheat and polish. The numbers are the same ones on the public GitHub journal. The site reads them from there, so the bar moves when the journal is updated. Next to it are links to follow the project on GitHub and to join the Discord.
