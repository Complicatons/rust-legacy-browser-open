Another pass over the wiki guides, checking every number against the original game's code and data.

## Guns roll their mod slots

Every gun you make or find rolls its own number of mod slots, anywhere from none up to 5 (4 on the bolt action), so two M4s can have different slots. Gun pages now show each gun's maximum, and the [Weapon mods](/wiki/guides/weapon-mods) guide explains it.

## Workbench times in the calculator

Standing at a workbench crafts everything three times faster. The [calculator](/wiki/calculator) now times a build at a workbench when any part of it needs one, so 12 explosive charges take about 19 minutes, not 56.

## Radiation zones

The [Radiation](/wiki/guides/radiation) guide now lists every zone with its strength and size. The Water Tank turned out to sit just outside the Oil Tanks zones, so it's no longer listed as radioactive.

## Checked and right

Armour values, weapon mods, smelting, healing and calories, fall damage and base costs all match the original game.
