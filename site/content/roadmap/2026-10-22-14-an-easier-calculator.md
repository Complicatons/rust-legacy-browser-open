The [calculator](/wiki/calculator) worked out the right numbers, but big totals like 8,292 sulfur were cut off and the page didn't say how to read it.

## In the order you play

The results now come as three numbered steps: **Gather** (everything you need to collect), **Smelt and burn** (ore for the furnace and wood for charcoal) and **Craft in this order**. A line under the first step says what the totals are for, like "For 12 × Explosive Charge". The two views are now called **From scratch** (down to what you gather) and **Recipe only** (just what the recipes take), and the totals say how many times you craft, how long it takes and whether you need a workbench.

## Numbers that fit

Every total in a set is sized for the longest one, so thousands of sulfur fit next to their icon on any screen.

## Straight from the top bar

Point at **Wiki** in the top bar and **Calculator** appears under it. On a phone, the menu has Calculator under Wiki.
