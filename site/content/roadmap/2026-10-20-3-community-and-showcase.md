## Discord

The community [Discord](https://discord.gg/your-invite) is set up from a script, so it can be rebuilt the same way every time. New members verify through Wick before they see more than the welcome, rules and announcements. There are staff channels by rank, level roles from Survivor to Legend, ping and region roles, a ticket panel for staff support, pinned guides in the help and bug report forums, and a weathered radiation plate as the server icon.

Two small bots tie it to the website. **#server-status** keeps one message up to date with the servers listed here: how many are online, players on each and the wipe number, in numbers only. **#updates** gets a post whenever a new update or a new roadmap entry is published on this site.

## The showcase

The public journal on GitHub got 49 new labelled screenshots and a gameplay tour recorded in the browser port with the game's own audio. The tour grew over four versions to **3 minutes 48 seconds**: the menu, the island from above, gathering, crafting, building a full hut with a door and a roof, guns, the bow, wildlife, a raid, two players together (against our own test server), a campfire at night, an airdrop and its crate. The clips on this page are cut from it.

The journal's README also gives an overall estimate of **76%** as of 20 October, weighted by area: the world 90%, survival and wildlife 95%, building 90%, combat 88%, menus 92%, online play 85%, the website 45%, anti-cheat 55%, polish 50%, and a first real server 10%.
