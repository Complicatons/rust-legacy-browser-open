With the island in place, the first day ended with the basic loop of Rust: hit things, collect resources, find food and stay alive.

## Gathering

Trees, rocks and wood piles work as in the original. The resource spawners refill the island over time, each node gives its original amounts, and the tree collision comes from the client's own tree meshes. Left click swings whatever tool is on your belt, and every swing costs calories, as it did in Legacy.

While wiring this up, the item export was found to be incomplete. Reading the right data bundle brought it to **245 item definitions and 108 blueprints**. The [items](/wiki/items) and [recipes](/wiki/recipes) in the wiki come from this same data.

## The animals

All eight original animals came across with their original models and animations: chickens, rabbits, boars, stags, wolves, bears, mutant wolves and mutant bears. Each one runs the original wildlife AI: wandering, fleeing, chasing, attacking and drowning if it strays into the sea. Animals can be killed and harvested, and they come back through the original spawn rules. The [animals page](/wiki/animals) lists them with their health and drops.

## Staying alive

The original metabolism arrived the same day: calories, bleeding, healing over time and the right-click menu to eat food or use bandages and medical kits. Player health is 100 as in the original, death shows the death screen, and you respawn through the original spawn points.
