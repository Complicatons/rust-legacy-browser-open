The second day turned the island from a place to explore into a place to live.

## Camps

All 19 original deployables came across: campfires, furnaces, storage boxes, workbenches, sleeping bags, beds and the rest. Placement follows the original checks: range, slope, spacing, stacking and the island's 13 no-build zones. A green or red ghost shows whether a spot is valid before you click.

Fires and furnaces burn and cook the way they did. Fuel burns on the original 60 and 30 second cycles, leaves charcoal, and raw meat cooks once the fire is hot enough. The original flames, smoke, sparks and firelight are drawn from the game's own particle data. Tap E to search, ignite or pick up, or hold E for the full option menu. Sleeping bags and beds let you respawn at your camp, with the original cooldowns.

## Building

The building system is the original one, piece for piece. Foundations snap to the same 2.5 m grid, pillars sit on the corners, and walls, doorways, windows, ceilings, stairs and ramps follow the original placement rules for all eight piece types. Doors, gates and window bars hang on the original hardpoints. Pieces carry the original armour: walls and pillars ignore melee, and a wooden door takes a tenth of melee damage.

The [building page](/wiki/building) in the wiki lists every piece and what it costs.
