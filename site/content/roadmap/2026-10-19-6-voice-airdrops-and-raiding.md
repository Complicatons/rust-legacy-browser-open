The fourth round had six pieces of work running in parallel. The [update from 19 October](/updates/2026-10-19-voice-airdrops-and-raiding) has a short version.

## Voice

Hold **V** to talk. The microphone is only used while the key is held and never when the page loads. The voice codec is the original one, and other players hear you nearby, fading out to 100 m, as in the original.

## Combat feedback

Hits show the original hit marker and sound, headshots have their own sound, damage shows the arcs pointing to where it came from, and the screen flashes red when you are hurt.

## Airdrops

The plane, the supply crate, its parachute, the landing smoke and the loot are all the original ones. Automatic drops need more than 50 players on a server, as in the original, so they never happen in single player. A Supply Signal calls one in.

## Quality and shadows

The original six quality levels, from Fastest to Fantastic, with sun shadows from Good up and the original post-processing: sun shafts, anti-aliasing, film grain and bloom. The default stays at the original Fastest.

## Raiding

Damage to buildings follows the original rules exactly. There is **no structural collapse** in the original: foundations, pillars and ceilings shrug off bullets, melee and explosives. Explosive charges needed per piece:

| Target | Charges |
|---|---|
| Wooden Door | 1 |
| Metal Door | 2 |
| Wood Wall, Doorway, Window | 2 |
| Wood Gate | 3 |
| Metal Wall, Doorway, Window | 4 |
| Wood Gateway | 5 |

The [raiding page](/wiki/raiding) in the wiki has every piece. Name tags over other players and use icons on things you can use also arrived, and keys can be rebound in Options (Use and the belt keys stay fixed, as in the original).
