Players asked how to raid, so the [wiki guides](/wiki/guides) now walk through it, and the other side of it too.

## The new guides

- **[Raiding step by step](/wiki/guides/raiding-step-by-step):** scouting a base, adding up the charges on every route in, the raid kit for 1 to 4 charges (sulfur, charcoal, fragments, fuel, leather, and what that means in ore and wood), when to go, and what's possible without explosives.
- **[Defending your base](/wiki/guides/defending-your-base):** what can and can't be broken, which upgrades cost a raider the most for the least, layering doors, keeping boxes out of the blast, decay and logging off safely.
- **[PvP basics](/wiki/guides/pvp-basics):** shots to kill for each weapon with and without Kevlar, headshots, recoil, bleeding and healing in a fight.
- **[Loot runs](/wiki/guides/loot-runs):** where the crates are, and how long you can stand at the centre of each radiation zone before reaching 500 rads, with and without a rad suit.

## Easier to find

The wiki's front page and the top of the guides list now show **recommended guides**: your first day, raiding, the raid plan, defence, PvP and loot runs. On phones they sit two to a row.

As always, the numbers come from the original game's data and code: the 10 seconds at the Warehouse Compound's centre, for example, combine its 3,000 rads a minute with the original rate at which rads wear off.
