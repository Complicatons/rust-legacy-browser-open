We went through how the original game showed animals and a few survival details, line by line, and fixed everything where ours differed.

## What changed

- **Hills.** On a server, animals leaned backwards going uphill. They now tilt the way the original drew them.
- **Coats.** Chickens on a server are roosters or one of two hens, picked the same way the original picked them, and wolves get one of their two coats. Before, every one looked the same.
- **Bites.** A wolf's bite and a bear's swipe now play to the end instead of being cut short, and an animal only changes its walk or run when its speed really changes.
- **Kills.** When you kill an animal, it drops straight away, lying flat on the slope, as in the original. Kills by someone else still show a moment later.
- **Speed.** An animal that stood still for a while no longer starts walking in slow motion; its legs match how fast it is really moving.
- **Sounds.** An animal's cry now follows the animal as it runs, instead of staying where it started.
- **Warm.** The "Warm" sign by a fire no longer flickers on a server.
- **Swings.** Your tool only does its pull-back after hitting something that can take damage, or a tree that still has wood, like the original.

## Still to check

These were checked against our test server. They still need a run on the original game server.
