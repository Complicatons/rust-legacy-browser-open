The [wiki](/wiki) listed every item, but it didn't say what happens when you put something in a fire. Now it does, straight from the original game data.

## On the item pages

- **[Raw Chicken Breast](/wiki/items/raw-chicken-breast)** cooks into Cooked Chicken Breast on a camp fire, one for one. In a furnace it burns to charcoal: a furnace is too hot for food.
- **[Sulfur Ore](/wiki/items/sulfur-ore)** and **[Metal Ore](/wiki/items/metal-ore)** smelt only in a furnace: 3–4 sulfur or 3–8 metal fragments per ore.
- **[Cloth](/wiki/items/cloth)** turns into leather in a furnace, one for one. A camp fire's cooking slots only take raw food.
- **[Wood](/wiki/items/wood)** leaves charcoal behind: 3 per wood on a camp fire, 2.5 in a furnace.
- Food now also lists the animals it comes from, so raw chicken shows every animal you can harvest it from.

## In the calculator

Cooked Chicken Breast is now in the [calculator](/wiki/calculator), with a "Cook on a camp fire" step and the raw chicken it takes. The **Into the fire** box shows the sulfur ore and metal ore to smelt, and how much wood to burn for the charcoal, on a camp fire or in a furnace.

The [Food and cooking](/wiki/guides/food-and-cooking) and [Smelting](/wiki/guides/smelting) guides were corrected to match: meat burns in a furnace, and cloth only turns into leather in a furnace.
