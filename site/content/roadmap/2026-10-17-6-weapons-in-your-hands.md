From the evening of 17 October through the 18th, the work moved to how the game feels in your hands. The [update from 18 October](/updates/2026-10-18-weapons-and-animation) has a short version.

## First person

Every held item now uses its original first-person model: 17 models covering the rock, hatchets, pick axe, torch, flare, every firearm, the hunting bow, grenades and the hand cannon. They play their original animations for deploying, swinging, firing, reloading and drawing the bow, and aiming down the sights lines up the real iron sights. The torch lights itself with its original flames and light. Other players' held items use the separate third-person models the original uses.

## The HUD and the camera

The original HUD came back: its sprites, its 12 px bitmap font, the health, food and status plaques with their original colours and thresholds. The camera got the original lazy weapon sway and the original head bob, which uses a spring system with curves for walking, strafing and aiming rather than a simple wobble. Shots and hits punch the view the way they did.

## Effects and attachments

Muzzle flashes and smoke come from the original effect data, with the brief light they cast. Bullets fly as the original tracer mesh, fade over distance, and leave the original impact particles, sounds and bullet holes on wood, metal, flesh and the ground. All four weapon mods work: the holo sight with its reticle, the silencer (quieter, no flash, no visible tracer, less damage and range), the flashlight (F) and the laser (G), which other players see too.
