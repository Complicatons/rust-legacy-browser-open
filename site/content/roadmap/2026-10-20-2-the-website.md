The old one-page site was replaced by this one in a single day, in six milestones, with a few follow-ups on the 21st.

## Accounts

Create an account with a username and email, or sign up with Steam or Discord and pick a username. Passwords are stored as slow hashes, sign-in locks for 15 minutes after 10 failures, and email changes need your current password. Password reset and email verification are ready and wait only for a mail provider.

## Title screen and servers

The [homepage](/) is the game's title screen: the menu, the original menu music once you click, and on capable desktops the live menu scene from the game. Quick join picks a server for you. The [server browser](/servers) has filters you can share as a link, a details panel and a page for each server.

## The Island

[The Island](/island) is a map of the island built from the original geometry, with three layers. The **aerial** layer is 49 top-down shots of our port stitched into one image at 0.5 m per pixel. The **modern** layer is drawn from the original buildings, roads and places: 357 building outlines, 13 named places, 12 radiation zones. On 21 October it gained the 80 loot crate spawns with their odds, 42 spawn zones for animals, mutants and resources, 13 no-build zones and the island's elevation. The **classic** layer is waiting for the community map (see What's coming).

## Wiki, updates and guide

The [wiki](/wiki) is generated from the game's own data: 137 items, 108 recipes, building costs, raid charges, animals, resources, places and radiation zones, each traced to the file it came from. Staff can write articles with a history of every change. [Updates](/updates) posts news, and [How to play](/guide) checks your browser and lists the controls. Every page works on a phone, even though the game itself needs a desktop.
