The website you are reading now runs on our own server in London, behind Cloudflare, at **your-domain.example**.

## What works

- **Every page:** the title screen, the island map, the wiki, updates, this roadmap and the server list.
- **Accounts:** you can register with a username and password, or sign in with Steam.
- **Opening-day sign-ups:** the "notify me" box on the homepage collects email addresses for when the servers open.
- **This roadmap in order:** on busy days with ten or more updates, the later ones used to show up among the first. They now appear in the order they were made.

## Not yet

- **No game servers are listed.** The game server machine is ordered and running, but the original server and our bridge are not installed on it yet.
- **Discord sign-in and account emails** (password reset, email checks) are switched off until they are set up.
