The original inventory made a small tap sound when you clicked a slot or a button, and a sound when crafting started. Our inventory was silent.

## Where the sounds come from

Both sounds are in the original game files, along with the list of what plays them:

- **Tap** plays when you click an inventory, belt, loot or armour slot, a camp fire or repair bench slot, a recipe in the crafting list, the crafting − and + buttons, Craft or Cancel, Repair, and the Close buttons.
- **Craft start** plays once when a craft actually begins.

Buttons the original kept switched off, such as Take All, stay silent.

## Buttons that give a little

The same original files also say how slots react under the mouse. Armour slots and the camp fire and repair bench slots shrink very slightly when you point at them and a little more when you press, for a fortieth of a second. The bag, belt and loot slots have the same effect switched off in the original, so they stay still in ours too.

## How it was checked

The test clicks real slots and buttons in the game and checks that each one played the original clip, that starting a craft played the craft sound exactly once, and that closing the inventory tapped.
