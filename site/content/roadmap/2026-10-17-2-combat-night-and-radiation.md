## Combat

Combat uses the original weapon data and rules rather than tuned guesses. Bullets travel along your aim with no added spread, damage rolls between each weapon's original minimum and maximum, and headshots multiply damage as they did. Weapons lose condition as you fire, broken weapons cannot be used, and every gun has its original fire rate, recoil, reload time and dry-fire click. Shotguns fire their original pellet spread.

The hunting bow draws for two seconds, tires after one, and fires arrows that drop over distance. F1 grenades have a 3 second fuse and bounce off the ground with the original physics material. The explosive charge sticks to the piece you place it on and goes off after 5 seconds, so two charges break a 1,000 hp wooden wall. Armour goes in the four armour slots and adds its original protection values.

## Night, cold and radiation

The original day and night cycle came next, with its calendar, moon phases and the way the sun and moon light the island. Night brings cold: clothing protects you, and a fire keeps you warm. The island's 12 original radiation zones build up exposure while you stand in them, worn radiation armour reduces it, and anti-radiation pills bring it back down. The original waterline check is in too: go too deep into the water and you die.

Later work replaced the first sky with the original sky and sea (see 19 October). The [radiation page](/wiki/radiation) lists every zone.
