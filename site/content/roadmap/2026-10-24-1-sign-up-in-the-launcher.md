To play with the desktop game you need an account on our website. Until now that meant going to the website first. Now the launcher has a **Create account** tab right next to **Sign in**.

## What changed

- **Sign up in the launcher.** Email, username and password, with the same rules as the website. The account works straight away, in the launcher and on the website.
- **Two ticks.** *Remember my email or username* fills it in next time. *Keep me signed in on this PC* skips the password next time. Your password itself is never saved; the launcher keeps a sign-in token locked to your Windows account instead, which you can sign out from the website.
- **A tidier window.** The launcher now sizes itself to each step instead of leaving empty space.
- **Bans follow the PC.** The launcher sends a fingerprint of the PC (never the raw details), so a banned player can't simply make a new account on the same machine.
- **An installer.** It installs for your Windows user only and needs no administrator rights.

## Back in the plan

The piece that lets the original game join a server as your website account was on hold. It's back in the plan, for servers whose owners run our server software. It hasn't been tried with the real game yet; that's the next test.
