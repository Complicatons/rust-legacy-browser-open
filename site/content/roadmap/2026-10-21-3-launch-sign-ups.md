Before the servers open there is now one page to share: the [launch page](/launch). It has one email box and a **Notify me** button. Leave your email and you'll hear first when the servers go live. Below the form, a Discord button takes you to the server for news, sneak peeks and the opening date.

## After you sign up

The page then asks if you want to create your account now. You need an account to log in and play on every server, and making it early means your username is yours before anyone else takes it. **Create account** opens the sign-up form with your email already filled in. **Maybe later** keeps you on the list and you can register any time.

## Your email

- Each address is stored once. Signing up again, or with different capitals, changes nothing.
- We only use it to tell you about opening day. Ask and we'll remove it.
- The form only accepts a few tries a minute from one address, and a hidden field catches simple spam bots.
- No email is sent yet. The list is kept until the opening-day announcement.

## Sharing the link

Paste the launch page or the homepage into Discord or a social site and it now shows a large preview card: the title over the game's own title screen, with **Get notified** or **Play now**. The cards are drawn by a build step with the site's fonts, so they stay in step with the site.

## For staff

Platform staff have a sign-ups page linked from their account: the total, how many signed up in the last day and week, the full list newest first, a CSV download and a copy-all button for the opening-day email, and a remove button for anyone who asks to be taken off.

## On the homepage too

The homepage now shows the same overall progress figure as the roadmap, as a slim bar above Quick join, and the opening-day sign-up box right under it. Leave your email there and it goes on the same list, with the same offer to make your account.

## Ready to send the opening-day email

The website can now send email. Once the domain and a mail provider are connected, staff write the opening-day announcement on the sign-ups page, send themselves a test, then send it to everyone on the list. It goes out one email at a time, and each one says why you're getting it and has your own unsubscribe link, which also works from the unsubscribe button in mail apps. The same switch turns on password reset and email confirmation for accounts.
