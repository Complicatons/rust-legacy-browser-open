The [roadmap](/roadmap) now has a search box between the totals and the timeline.

## How it works

- Every word you type has to appear somewhere in an entry: its title, summary, text, numbers or picture captions. Case and word order don't matter, and "signups" finds "sign-ups".
- Dates work too: "21 oct" or "october 21" shows that day's entries.
- Days with no matches drop out of the timeline, the date list and the day buttons, and the box says how many entries match, such as "5 of 92 entries".
- The search stays in the address (`/roadmap?q=mutant`), so a search can be shared. Esc clears it, and "/" jumps to the box from anywhere on the page.
