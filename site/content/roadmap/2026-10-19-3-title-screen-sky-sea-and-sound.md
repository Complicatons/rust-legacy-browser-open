This was a big-systems day, with four pieces of work running side by side. The [update from 19 October](/updates/2026-10-19-menu-sky-and-sea) has a short version.

## The original front end

Opening the game now shows the original **main menu** over its own scene, slightly blurred as in the original. **Options** has the original rows: mouse sensitivity with the original formula, invert Y, volume and music, render quality and water reflections, plus a short browser section for field of view, fullscreen and the mouse release key. The **server browser** has the original six categories, sorting, pages, history and favourites, lists the servers from this website, and joins on a double click. Loading shows the original loading screen with its real progress. Developer tools only appear when asked for.

## Trees, sky and sea

Trees now follow the island's original settings: full models up to 214 m, the 60 nearest at full detail, and flat stand-ins out to 2,000 m that are redrawn as you turn. A forest view went from 6.4 million triangles to 1.2 million. The sky is the original TOD sky: atmosphere, stars, the sun, moon phases, clouds and fog. The sea is the original **Water4** water with its waves, foam and reflections.

## Sound

The island sounds like Legacy again: footsteps that change with the ground, animal hooves, the bone break when you fall too far, the Geiger counter, 17 day and night forest ambience loops and the original music track. The original player death sound turned out to be silent, and there is no jump sound in the original either, so neither was added.
