## Health

A new **Health** page in the host app shows the last 24 hours or 7 days: players in game, the game server's CPU and memory, how quickly it answers, and errors in the logs. Freezes and restarts are marked on every graph. Point at a graph to read the numbers at that moment, or show them as a table.

## Discord alerts

Owners paste a Discord webhook and choose what they hear about: a crash, a freeze and its recovery, a part that stopped, the server back online, anti-cheat flags above a chosen severity, player reports, finished restarts and wipes, and low disk space. Alerts that happen together arrive as one message.

## Player history

Clicking a player shows their first and last visit, total play time, the names they've used, their flags, bans, kicks and reports on that server.
