The [wiki guides](/wiki/guides) grew from 28 to 35, all on small things that are easy to get wrong.

## The new guides

- **[Controls](/wiki/guides/controls):** every default key, how to change them in Options or with `input.bind`, and the few keys the original never let you change.
- **[Moving around](/wiki/guides/moving-around):** walking at 4 m/s, sprinting at 8, crouching at 2.2, jumping 1.5 m, and how crouching cuts a gun's recoil.
- **[Melee weapons and tools](/wiki/guides/melee-weapons):** damage, seconds per swing, hits to kill, and why a starving player swings at half speed.
- **[Bows and arrows](/wiki/guides/bows-and-arrows):** the 2-second draw, the 8-second limit on holding it, 75 damage per arrow, and arrows needed for each animal.
- **[Grenades](/wiki/guides/grenades):** the 3-second fuse that starts when you throw, the 6 m blast, and why cover cuts the damage to a tenth.
- **[Stack sizes](/wiki/guides/stack-sizes):** what stacks to 250, 12, 10, 5 or 1, and how splitting works.
- **[What a base costs](/wiki/guides/base-costs):** every piece in raw wood and metal fragments, and 410 wood for a first hut.

As before, every number comes from the original game's data and code. One finding is new to the wiki: standing at a workbench makes **all** crafting three times faster, not only the recipes that need it.

## More pictures

Six guides that had no pictures now have them: armour, repairing, defences, chat and the console, fall damage, and healing. Guides also no longer show the same picture twice at the top.
