The wiki has a new [resource calculator](/wiki/calculator). Pick what you want to make from the list of everything craftable, set how many, and it shows what you need to gather.

## What it shows

- **Base resources:** the calculator works all the way down the recipes. Two explosive charges and a metal door come to 1,382 sulfur, 1,232 charcoal, 516 metal fragments, 120 animal fat, 60 cloth and 10 leather. Switch to **Direct ingredients** to see only what goes into each item itself.
- **Ore to smelt:** how much sulfur ore and metal ore that takes, from a lucky to an unlucky furnace.
- **Crafting order:** every craft step in order, with the ingredients, how long it takes and whether it needs a workbench. Gunpowder comes first, then explosives and flares, then the charges.
- **Totals:** the number of crafts, the total crafting time and anything left over when a recipe makes more than one, like arrows.

Things used in more than one recipe, like gunpowder, are added up across your whole list before it rounds up to whole crafts, so the totals match what you'd really craft.

## Sharing

Your list is kept in the page address, so **Copy link** gives you a link that opens the same build, ready to send to your team before a raid. Every number comes from the original game's recipes, the same data as the wiki's [Crafting](/wiki/recipes) section.
