Firearms now look and sound like the original game, using the effects recovered from its files.

## Firing

- **Muzzle flash:** the original flash and smoke particles at the weapon's muzzle, with a short light fade. Silencers hide the flash.
- **Tracers:** the original tracer mesh and texture, with its randomised width and length and its distance fade.
- **Impacts:** the original bullet particles for wood, metal and default surfaces, impact sounds and the bullet-hole decal.

## Attachments

All four weapon mods work: holo sight, laser sight, flashlight mod and silencer. A silenced weapon uses its quiet sound and has no tracer or flash. The Shotgun keeps the original's visible tracer even when silenced.

## Player animation

Players move with the original animation blending: walking, strafing, crouching, jumping and landing, with armed poses for twelve held item types.
