## Chat

The original chat layout. Press **Enter** or **T** to talk. Lines stay for 16 seconds, with the original limits of one message a second and 128 characters.

## Console

Press **F1** for the original console window. Commands the game doesn't run on your side are sent to the server, and its replies show in the console.

## World shaders

The world now uses the original lighting. The game rendered with Unity 4 deferred lighting, and its maths now runs in the browser for terrain, rocks, roads, buildings and trees.

Nights are nearly black, as the original data gives. Bring a torch.
