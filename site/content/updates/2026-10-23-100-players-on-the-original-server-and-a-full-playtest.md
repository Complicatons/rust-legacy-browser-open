## On the original server

- **100 browser players at once** on the original 2014 server, with a 101 ms ping. It ran for **3 hours 17 minutes without a drop**, was stopped and started again mid-game, and kept everyone connected with half a second of added lag.
- **Test bots now play the whole game** against the server: they craft, build a base, lock a door, cook, smelt, raid, hunt, fight and loot each other. 47 of 48 online test rows pass.
- **Other players and animals move with the original's timing**, from the same delay behind the server to the same smoothing over gaps.

## A full playtest

We played the whole game twice, alone and online, from the menu to raiding a base. Everything that behaved differently from the original is fixed:

- **Repairing with wood works** offline, and a save closed on the death screen now starts a fresh character instead of a walking corpse.
- **You keep your blueprints when you die**, and seven more item and crafting rules match the original.
- **Walking, sprinting and jumping** follow the original rules, from slowing down uphill to the short stumble after a landing.
- **Gunfire, grenades and ragdolls**: eleven small differences fixed from the original code.
- **Animals** lean the right way on hills, wear their proper coats, finish their bites and die on the spot.
- **Spike walls, fires and the repair bench** follow the original rules, and the repair bench shows what a repair costs.
- **Chat and the F1 console work with your inventory open**, and Escape, the belt keys and dragging behave as in the original.
- **Mutants drop their loot sacks**, wood piles and ore burst as they shrink, items glide in the inventory, and the revolver sways like the original.
- **The ground and the laser sight**: terrain detail is chosen the original's way, and the laser draws its beam and dot exactly as the original did.

## Fair play

- **You don't need Steam to play.** A ban now follows the person, not just the account, and admins can take a screenshot of what a suspected cheater sees.
- **The anti-cheat checks melee swings, arrows and hits for walls in the way**, follows every door, and spots players hovering in mid-air.

## For server owners

The **host app** now installs from **one download** and runs as its own Windows service. It has pages for players, cheat evidence, world saves, linked accounts and updates, with one-click roll back.
