## Voice

Hold **V** to talk. Your microphone is only used while the key is held and never when the page loads. Other players hear you nearby, fading out over 100 m, as in the original.

## Airdrops

The plane, supply crate, parachute and landing smoke. As in the original, automatic drops need more than 50 players on the server. A **Supply Signal** calls one in.

## Raiding

The original damage rules for doors, walls and every other piece. There is no structural collapse in the original, and foundations, pillars and ceilings are immune to explosives.

| Target | Explosive charges |
|---|---|
| Wooden Door | 1 |
| Metal Door | 2 |
| Wood Wall, Doorway, Window | 2 |
| Wood Gate | 3 |
| Metal Wall, Doorway, Window | 4 |
| Wood Gateway | 5 |

The [raiding page](/wiki/raiding) in the wiki has every piece.
